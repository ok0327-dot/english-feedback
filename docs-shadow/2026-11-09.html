2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


① 계절 변화 말하기

❌ As Said: "It's coming winter time."
✅ Natural: "Winter is coming." / "Winter is on its way."
💎 Professional: "It looks like winter is just around the corner."
💡 코칭 포인트:

In English, the subject (winter) usually comes first, followed by "is coming." "Just around the corner" is a polished idiom meaning something will happen very soon.

→ 영어에서는 주어(winter)를 먼저 말하고 "is coming"을 붙이는 것이 자연스럽습니다. "just around the corner"는 '곧 다가온다'는 뜻의 세련된 관용 표현입니다.


② 아파트 게시판 설명하기

❌ As Said: "So if this apartment have a so big event, the apartment show the community board and we obtain something."
✅ Natural: "If there's a big event in our apartment, they post it on the community board, so we can find out about it."
💎 Professional: "Any major events in our complex are announced on the community board, so residents always stay informed."
💡 코칭 포인트:

"Obtain" sounds like receiving a physical item. For information, "find out about" or "stay informed" is much more natural. Also, notices are "posted on" a board, not "shown."

→ "obtain"은 물건을 '손에 넣는다'는 느낌입니다. 정보를 알게 되는 상황에서는 "find out about"이나 "stay informed"가 훨씬 자연스럽습니다. 게시판에 공지를 올리는 것은 "post (on)"라고 표현합니다.


③ 처음 참여할 때의 낯섦 표현하기

❌ As Said: "Everything is fresh and I don't know how to do."
✅ Natural: "Everything is new to me, and I don't know what to do."
💎 Professional: "As a first-timer, I'm still unfamiliar with the process, so a welcoming atmosphere really helps."
💡 코칭 포인트:

"Fresh" describes food or air, not unfamiliar situations. Use "new to me." Also, "how to do" needs an object ("how to do it"), so "what to do" is the natural choice.

→ "fresh"는 음식이나 공기가 '신선하다'고 할 때 씁니다. 낯선 상황에는 "new to me"를 씁니다. "how to do"는 뒤에 목적어가 있어야 하므로("how to do it"), 여기서는 "what to do"가 자연스럽습니다.


④ 강변 쓰레기 줍기 말하기

❌ As Said: "I'm putting and gathering trash in riverside."
✅ Natural: "I pick up trash along the riverside."
💎 Professional: "I'd love to join a riverside cleanup and help keep the walking trail clean."
💡 코칭 포인트:

Earlier you said "I put in the trash in the river," which can sound like you're putting trash *into* the river, the opposite of what you meant! The natural phrase is "pick up trash," as your tutor taught you. Also add "along the" or "by the" before "riverside."

→ 앞서 말한 "I put in the trash in the river"는 '강에 쓰레기를 넣는다'는 정반대의 뜻으로 들릴 수 있습니다. 튜터가 알려준 대로 쓰레기를 '줍는다'는 "pick up trash"로 말하세요. "riverside" 앞에는 "along the"나 "by the"를 붙입니다.



3. 🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)


Category: to-infinitive form (to + base verb, NOT to + -ing / noun)


오늘 수업에서 "to" 뒤에 동사원형 대신 -ing 형태나 명사를 붙이는 실수가 5회 반복되었습니다. 특히 'love to', 'possible to', 'way to' 뒤에서 자주 나타났습니다.


❌ 원문 (As Said)✅ 교정 (Corrected)
"I love to child and children.""I love children." / "I love to work with children."
"Before I said I love to children's and I love to teaching children.""I said before that I love children and I love to teach them."
"It is possible to gathering volunteering people.""It's possible to gather volunteers."
"...that is good way to attending volunteer event like the cleanup event.""...that's a good reason to attend volunteer events like the cleanup."

📌 핵심 규칙: "to" 뒤에는 동사원형(to teach, to gather, to attend)이 옵니다. 명사를 쓰고 싶다면 to를 빼고 "I love + 명사(children)"처럼 말하세요.


🔁 따라 읽기

🗣️ "I love to teach children."
🗣️ "It's possible to gather volunteers through social media."
🗣️ "Meeting kind people is a good reason to attend volunteer events."


4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)


① give back /ɡɪv bæk/ (phrasal verb)

뜻: (사회·지역사회에) 환원하다, 보답하다
실전 예문: "Our company encourages employees to give back to the local community."

② stop by /stɑːp baɪ/ (phrasal verb)

뜻: 잠깐 들르다
실전 예문: "Feel free to stop by my desk if you have any questions."

③ sign up (for) /saɪn ʌp/ (phrasal verb)

뜻: (이름을 올려) 신청하다, 등록하다
실전 예문: "I signed up for the company volunteer program this month."

④ enriching /ɪnˈrɪtʃɪŋ/ (adjective)

뜻: (경험이) 유익한, 삶을 풍요롭게 하는
실전 예문: "Mentoring new employees has been a really enriching experience."

⑤ purposeful /ˈpɜːrpəsfəl/ (adjective)

뜻: 의미 있는, 분명한 목적이 있는
실전 예문: "I prefer purposeful meetings with a clear agenda."


5. 📝 실전 복습 챌린지 (Practice)


Q1. (쉬움) 동네 푸드뱅크 봉사에 참여하고 싶다고 말해 보세요. (get involved with)

💡 힌트: "I like to"는 '평소 ~하는 걸 좋아한다'는 뜻입니다. '~하고 싶다'는 "I'd like to"로 말합니다.
✅ 모범 답안: "I'd like to get involved with the local food bank."

Q2. (보통) 동료에게 봉사 신청 방법을 알려 주세요. "온라인으로 신청하거나 토요일에 봉사 테이블에 들르면 돼요." (sign up, stop by)

✅ 모범 답안: "You can sign up online or just stop by the volunteer table on Saturday."

Q3. (어려움) "아이들을 가르치는 건 저에게 정말 유익한 경험이에요. 그래서 주말마다 아이들을 가르치는 봉사를 하고 싶어요." (enriching, would love to + 동사원형)

✅ 모범 답안: "Teaching children is a really enriching experience for me, so I'd love to volunteer as a tutor for kids on weekends."


6. ✨ 자신감 충전 & 내일의 미션 (Confidence & Mission)


✨ Good Job

> "Yeah, it is enriching for me."


방금 배운 새 단어 enriching을 자신의 경험(아이들 가르치기)에 바로 연결해 정확한 문장으로 말했습니다. 단어를 외우는 데서 그치지 않고, 튜터에게 "It is enriching for me, right?"라고 확인한 뒤 직접 적용했습니다. 이것이 어휘를 '내 것'으로 만드는 가장 좋은 방법입니다. 👏
