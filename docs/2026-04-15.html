4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)

* 뜻: 주의를 산만하게 하는

* 실전 예문: During a presentation, unnecessary gestures can be distracting to the audience.


* deplete /dɪˈpliːt/ (동사)

* 뜻: 고갈시키다, 격감시키다

* 실전 예문: A monotonous voice can deplete the audience's energy and interest.


* pizzazz /pɪˈzæz/ (명사)

* 뜻: 활기, 매력, 생기; 열정

* 실전 예문: Her presentation lacked a certain pizzazz, making it less memorable.


* monotonous /məˈnɒtənəs/ (형용사)

* 뜻: 단조로운, 지루한

* 실전 예문: To avoid a monotonous delivery, vary your tone and pace during the speech.


* glossophobia /ˌɡlɒsəˈfoʊbiə/ (명사)

* 뜻: 대중 연설 공포증

* 실전 예문: Many professionals suffer from glossophobia, but it can be managed with practice and techniques.
