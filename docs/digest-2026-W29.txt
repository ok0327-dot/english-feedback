1. I said: "That is enjoyable for my life."
   Natural: "It's enjoyable for me."
2. I said: "So if the subject is changed at the time, the verb also changes, right?"
   Natural: "So if the subject changes, the verb also changes, right?"
3. I said: "people in our country, everyone, doesn't like that. They hate that."
   Natural: "In our country, everyone dislikes them."
4. I said: "our country is under the category of dogs."
   Natural: "Dogs are the most common pets in our country."
5. I said: "I have some worry about..."
   Natural: "I have some concerns about..."
6. I said: "it is hard to have more happier emotion."

11. I said: "So she, yeah, and she is a little late to recovery ."
