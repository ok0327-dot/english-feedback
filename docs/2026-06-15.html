2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)

Joey님이 말씀하신 문장 중 의미는 통하지만, 원어민이 들었을 때 좀 더 자연스럽게 들릴 수 있는 표현들을 함께 살펴볼게요.


1. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "This weekend is also nice because I shared my time with my family."

* ✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "I had a nice weekend because I spent time with my family."

* 💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "My weekend was pleasant as I had the opportunity to spend quality time with my family."

* 💡 코칭 포인트: Using 'spent time' is more common and natural when referring to how you used your time with someone. 'Shared my time' implies dividing your time among different people or activities, which isn't quite the nuance here. → 'spent time'은 누군가와 시간을 보냈다는 것을 나타낼 때 더 흔하고 자연스러운 표현입니다. 'shared my time'은 여러 사람이나 활동에 시간을 나누어 썼다는 뉘앙스를 풍기므로, 여기서는 적절하지 않습니다.


2. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "But this time is the lunch time."

* ✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "But it was during lunchtime." 또는 "But it was lunchtime."

* 💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "However, the event coincided with our lunch break."

* 💡 코칭 포인트: When referring to a specific period of time like "lunchtime," "it was during lunchtime" or simply "it was lunchtime" is more idiomatic. "This time is the lunchtime" sounds a bit like a direct translation. → "점심시간"과 같은 특정 기간을 언급할 때는 "it was during lunchtime" 또는 간단히 "it was lunchtime"이 더 자연스러운 표현입니다. "This time is the lunchtime"은 직역처럼 들립니다.


3. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "So I'm not sure if it is right to understand or not."

* ✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "So I'm not sure if I'm understanding it correctly."

* 💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "Therefore, I'm seeking clarification to ensure my comprehension is accurate."

* 💡 코칭 포인트: To express uncertainty about understanding something, "if I'm understanding it correctly" is the most natural and common phrasing. "Right to understand" is not typically used in this context. → 무언가를 제대로 이해하고 있는지 불확실할 때, "if I'm understanding it correctly"가 가장 자연스럽고 흔한 표현입니다. "Right to understand"는 이 맥락에서 일반적으로 사용되지 않습니다.

4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)

오늘 대화에서 나온 표현들과 비즈니스 환경에서 유용하게 쓰일 수 있는 어휘들을 정리해 보았습니다.


1. discrepancy /dɪˈskrɛpənsi/ (명사)

* 뜻: 불일치, 차이

* 실전 예문: We found a significant discrepancy between the reported expenses and the actual receipts.


2. audit /ˈɔːdɪt/ (명사/동사)

* 뜻: (회계) 감사; 감사를 하다

* 실전 예문: Our team is preparing for the annual financial audit next month.


3. coincide /ˌkoʊɪnˈsaɪd/ (동사)

* 뜻: 동시에 일어나다, 일치하다

* 실전 예문: The project deadline unfortunately coincides with my vacation plans.


4. allocate /ˈæləkeɪt/ (동사)

* 뜻: 할당하다, 배분하다

* 실전 예문: We need to allocate more resources to the marketing department for the new campaign.


5. prudent /ˈpruːdənt/ (형용사)

* 뜻: 신중한, 현명한

* 실전 예문: Making prudent financial decisions is crucial for long-term investment success.
