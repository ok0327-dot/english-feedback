💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


1.

❌ As Said: "On the weekend, I gave the time to my wife."
✅ Natural: "Over the weekend, I gave my wife some time to herself."
💎 Professional: "I gave my wife some personal time over the weekend so she could travel with her friends."
💡 코칭 포인트: "give the time" sounds vague — adding "to herself" or "personal time" makes it clear you mean free, private time. ("the time"은 막연하게 들립니다. "some time to herself / personal time"라고 하면 '혼자만의 자유 시간'이라는 뜻이 명확해집니다.)

2.

❌ As Said: "I have some worry about that movement because my wife doesn't like the wind."
✅ Natural: "I have some concerns about the move because my wife doesn't like the wind."
💎 Professional: "I do have a few reservations about relocating, since my wife isn't fond of the wind."
💡 코칭 포인트: Native speakers say "the move," not "that movement" (which means physical motion). Use "concerns / reservations" instead of "worry" for a smoother, more adult tone. ("movement"은 '움직임'이라 어색합니다. 이사는 "the move", 걱정은 "concerns/reservations"가 자연스럽습니다.)

3.

❌ As Said: "My dream is to live in a house, but the reality is to live in an apartment."
✅ Natural: "My dream is to live in a house, but realistically we'll end up in an apartment."
💎 Professional: "Ideally I'd live in a house, but in reality an apartment is the more practical choice."
💡 코칭 포인트: The "dream vs. reality" contrast is great — just swap the stiff "the reality is to..." for "realistically..." or "in reality..." to sound more native. ('꿈 vs 현실' 대비는 훌륭합니다. 다만 "the reality is to..."보다 "realistically..." / "in reality..."가 더 원어민답습니다.)

🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)


Category: verb tense (past vs. present consistency in narration)


Joey는 어휘와 표현이 풍부하지만, 과거 이야기를 하다가 현재형으로 미끄러지는 패턴이 반복적으로 나타났습니다. 과거에 일어난 일을 묘사할 때는 문장 전체를 과거시제로 통일해야 합니다.


❌ 원문 (As Said)✅ 교정 (Correction)
"Maybe I'm driving sometime and I find a new building house and I love that, so I purchased it.""I was driving one day, and I found a newly built house, and I loved it, so I purchased it."
"Swimming slowly and eat delicious food and talked so many things.""We swam slowly, ate delicious food, and talked about so many things."
"The government gives us so many taxes. We paid so many taxes.""The government charged us heavy taxes, and we paid a lot in taxes."

📌 핵심 규칙: 과거 사건을 이야기할 때는 한 문장 안의 모든 동사를 과거형으로 맞춘다 (drive → drove, find → found, eat → ate, give → gave).


🔁 따라 읽기:

🗣️ "I was driving one day, and I found a newly built house, and I loved it, so I purchased it."
