2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


1) ❌ As Said: On the weekend, I gave the time to my wife.

✅ Natural: I spent time with my wife on the weekend.

💎 Professional: I dedicated my weekend to my wife.

💡 Coaching Point: "Give the time to someone"은 한국어에서 영어로 직역한 표현으로, 영어에서는 부자연스럽게 들립니다. "spend time with" 또는 "dedicate time to"를 사용하면 더 자연스럽고 관용적인 표현이 됩니다.


2) ❌ As Said: And in my case, I played out with my son.

✅ Natural: As for me, I spent time with my son. / I took my son out.

💎 Professional: I spent the weekend with my son.

💡 Coaching Point: "Played out"은 누군가와 여가 시간을 보냈다는 것을 표현하는 표준적인 관용구가 아닙니다. "Spent time with"는 이 의미를 전달하는 다양하고 흔하게 쓰이는 표현입니다. "Took someone out"은 특정 장소에 함께 갔다는 의미를 내포합니다.


3) ❌ As Said: So Saturday, we visited. We went to a hotel pool...

✅ Natural: On Saturday, we went to a hotel pool...

💎 Professional: On Saturday, we visited a hotel pool...

💡 Coaching Point: "we visited. We went to..."와 같이 두 번에 나누어 말하는 것은 약간 중복되고 끊기는 느낌을 줍니다. "we went to" 또는 "we visited"처럼 간결하게 하나로 합치면 문장이 더 부드럽게 이어집니다.


4) ❌ As Said: Yes, that is not busy, just because that is relaxing swimming, not fast.

✅ Natural: No, it wasn't busy; it was relaxing swimming, not fast-paced.

💎 Professional: It wasn't crowded at all; we enjoyed a leisurely swim rather than a fast-paced one.

💡 Coaching Point: 질문이 "Was it busy?"였으므로 "Yes, that is not busy"보다는 "No, it wasn't busy"가 더 자연스럽습니다. 또한 "relaxing swimming, not fast"는 "relaxing swimming, not fast-paced" 또는 "a leisurely swim"으로 표현하는 것이 더 자연스럽습니다.


5) ❌ As Said: ...the Korean people have had experience about the price because in Seoul, apartments are always going higher, so they believe the value is high and they don't go below, just going higher.

✅ Natural: ...Korean people have experienced price increases in Seoul apartments, so they believe the value is high and will continue to rise.

💎 Professional: ...Korean people have observed a consistent upward trend in Seoul apartment prices, leading them to believe the value is robust and will appreciate further.

💡 Coaching Point: "experience about the price"는 "experienced price increases" 또는 "observed a trend in prices"로 더 자연스럽게 표현할 수 있습니다. "don't go below, just going higher"는 "will continue to rise" 또는 "will appreciate further"와 같이 간결하게 표현하는 것이 좋습니다.
