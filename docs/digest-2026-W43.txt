## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "Yeah, it's a start, starting everything."
   Natural: "It's the start of a new week."
2. I said: "You were able to transfer your tickets or change it to something?"
   Natural: "Were you able to transfer or change your tickets?"
3. I said: "I think it's from British, from the UK, from there."
   Natural: "I think it's from British influence / from the UK."
4. I said: "Is the USA and Australia and United Kingdom and New Zealand gas station open have convenience stores?"
   Natural: "Do gas stations in the USA, Australia, the UK, and New Zealand have convenience stores?"
5. I said: "And the cashier handle both gas payment and store purchase?"
   Natural: "And do cashiers handle both gas payments and store purchases?"
6. I said: "I don't distinguish the quality of the gasoline."
   Natural: "I can't really tell the difference in gasoline quality."
7. I said: "It makes it more easier."
   Natural: "It makes it easier."
8. I said: "Is it possible to pre-talk?"
   Natural: "Can we just chat informally first?"
9. I said: "It is only I have experience to rent a house in Korea"
   Natural: "I've only had experience renting a house in Korea."
10. I said: "I like to do interior in some space."
   Natural: "I enjoy doing interior design in various spaces."
11. I said: "because it will start autumn."
   Natural: "because autumn is starting."
12. I said: "every tree has changed color, changed leaves."
   Natural: "the leaves on every tree have changed color."
13. I said: "So usually the people gather and live in our apartment."
   Natural: "So usually people live in apartment complexes."
14. I said: "This weekend, ah, this day we traveled to a small shop, small restaurant near my house."
   Natural: "This past weekend, we traveled to a small shop..."
15. I said: "And other things, just buy, just look around and check that we need this item."
   Natural: "For other things, we just look around and buy what we need."
16. I said: "I guess he's the first time in this shopping center , a supermarket."
   Natural: "I guess it's his first time at this supermarket."
