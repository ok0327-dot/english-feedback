2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


① 평범한 하루 소개하기

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "Today, it's a normal Wednesday, the middle of week. So, maybe it is, I have some routine of my normal days."
✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "It's just a regular Wednesday, the middle of the week. I'm sticking to my usual routine today."
💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "It's a typical midweek day for me, so I'll be following my usual routine."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Stick to my usual routine" is a ready-made chunk. You don't need "maybe it is" to warm up. Start right away with "It's just a…", which sounds relaxed and confident. Also, say "the middle of the week" as one chunk.

→ "stick to my usual routine(늘 하던 루틴대로 하다)"는 통째로 외워 두면 좋은 덩어리예요. "maybe it is"처럼 말을 고르는 군더더기 없이 "It's just a…"로 바로 시작하면 여유 있고 자신감 있게 들려요. "the middle of the week"도 한 덩어리로 입에 붙여 두세요.


② 스트레스 신호 말하기

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "I think if I have a headache or my eye was sick, sore my eyes, and this signal is that I had so many stress."
✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "When I get a headache or my eyes feel sore, that's a sign I'm under a lot of stress."
💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "Headaches and eye strain are usually my first signs that I'm under too much stress."
💡 코칭 포인트: Stress isn't something you count one by one, so say "a lot of stress," not "many stress." "That's a sign (that) ~" and "I'm under a lot of stress" are two handy chunks.

→ stress는 한 개, 두 개로 세는 말이 아니라서 many 대신 a lot of stress라고 해요. "~하면 그게 신호예요"는 "That's a sign ~", "스트레스를 많이 받고 있어요"는 "I'm under a lot of stress"를 한 덩어리로 쓰면 돼요. 눈이 아플 때는 "my eye was sick" 대신 "my eyes feel sore"라고 하면 자연스러워요.


③ 스트레스 푸는 방법 말하기

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "So just I release my stress to yoga, to yoga in the weekends, or to swimming in a hotel pool, or walk the near park."
✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "I usually relieve stress by doing yoga on weekends, swimming in a hotel pool, or taking a walk in a nearby park."
💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "To manage stress, I make time for yoga, swimming, and walks in the park on weekends."
💡 코칭 포인트: Native speakers usually say "relieve stress" rather than "release stress." Use "by + -ing" to list your methods. It keeps the sentence clean and easy to follow. Also, "on weekends" and "a nearby park" are fixed chunks.

→ 원어민은 스트레스를 '푼다'고 할 때 release보다 relieve stress를 더 많이 써요. 방법을 나열할 때는 "by + -ing(~하면서, ~함으로써)" 덩어리를 쓰면 문장이 깔끔하게 이어져요: by doing yoga, swimming, taking a walk. "주말에"는 on weekends, "근처 공원"은 a nearby park로 통째로 기억하세요.


④ 발음 피드백 요청하기

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "Is there some different pronunciation in the word?"
✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "Did I mispronounce any of the words?"
💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "Could you point out any words I didn't pronounce correctly?"
💡 코칭 포인트: "Mispronounce" says exactly what you mean in one word. "Point out" is a polite, professional way to ask for corrections.

→ "mispronounce(잘못 발음하다)" 한 단어로 의도가 딱 전달돼요. "point out(짚어 주다)"은 피드백을 정중하게 부탁할 때 회의에서도 쓰기 좋은 표현이에요.



3. 🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)


Category: verb form (redundant 'be' before a main verb)


🎯 오늘의 패턴: "~에 도움이 돼"를 말할 때 is/are와 help를 한꺼번에 붙이는 습관


Joey는 오늘 "도움이 된다", "향상시킨다"를 말할 때 is/are와 help, step up 같은 '하다' 말을 겹쳐 쓰는 일이 반복됐어요. 한국어 "도움이 돼"의 '이 돼'를 is로 옮기려다 생긴 습관이에요.


❌ 원문 (As Said)✅ 교정 (Corrected)
"that both activities are help to my body and my mental."Both activities help my body and mind. / Both activities are good for my body and mind.
"it is help to cure of the Alzheimer's."It can help protect against Alzheimer's.
"it is helped to become our body and mind strong."It helps make our body and mind strong.
"it is a step up our life and our body immune system."It boosts our quality of life and our immune system.

📌 핵심 규칙: help는 혼자서도 '도움이 돼'라는 뜻이에요. 앞에 is를 붙이지 말고 "It helps ~"로 바로 말하세요. is를 꼭 쓰고 싶다면 "It is good for ~" 덩어리로 바꾸면 돼요.

(✔ It helps me. ✔ It is good for me. ✘ It is help me.)

※ "마음, 정신"은 my mental이 아니라 my mind라고 해요: my body and mind.


🔁 따라 읽기:

🗣️ "Both activities help my body and mind."
🗣️ "It helps make our body and mind strong."
🗣️ "A little stress is good for my work performance."


4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)


1. perception /pərˈsepʃən/ (noun)
뜻: 인식, 사람들이 무언가를 바라보는 시각
실전 예문: *The client's perception of our audit process has improved this year.*

2. stimulate /ˈstɪmjəleɪt/ (verb)
뜻: 자극하다, 활성화하다
※ 오늘 교정받은 발음: stimulate예요. T 소리를 꼭 살리세요! T를 빼고 simulate라고 하면 '흉내 내다, 모의실험하다'라는 전혀 다른 단어로 들려요.
실전 예문: *Lower energy prices could stimulate demand for imports.*

3. relieve /rɪˈliːv/ (verb)
뜻: (고통이나 스트레스를) 덜어 주다, 풀다
실전 예문: *A short walk after lunch helps me relieve stress during audit season.*

4. metabolism /məˈtæbəlɪzəm/ (noun)
뜻: 신진대사, 음식을 에너지로 바꾸는 몸의 과정
실전 예문: *Regular exercise keeps my metabolism active even with a desk job.*

5. equipped /ɪˈkwɪpt/ (adjective)
뜻: (능력이나 장비를) 갖춘
※ 끝의 -pped는 '프드'가 아니라 짧은 '트' 소리예요. '이큅트'처럼 한 번에 끊어 말하세요.
실전 예문: *Our team is well equipped to handle the new reporting standards.*
