알렉스|en|"That was such an enjoyable time with my family, with my son."

민지|en|But in English, the verb itself must show the time.
알렉스|en|Yes. If the moment is finished, the verb must change: "is" becomes "was."

알렉스|en|Correct. After a subject like "I," we need a full verb, not the -ing form alone.

알렉스|en|Because it is one solution among many, not a specific one we both already know.

알렉스|en|"The" means we both know exactly which one. "A" means one of several, new to the listener.
민지|en|So "a solution" introduces it, and "the solution" points to a known one.
알렉스|en|Exactly. Think of "the" as a shared spotlight, and "a" as a first introduction.
민지|en|That is a beautiful rule of thumb: a to introduce, the to point.
알렉스|en|And one more small thing: "solution for" is more natural than "solution to" here.
민지|en|Good catch. Prepositions are sneaky too.
