알렉스|en|The biggest pattern this week was the news trap. Joey, you said: Are there any good news?
민지|en|And the natural version is: Is there any good news? Just one small swap. Are becomes is.
알렉스|en|Now, why does this happen? In Korean, the word for news sounds and feels like it could be plural. So your brain says, plural, use are.
민지|ko|한국어로 뉴스라고 하면 왠지 여러 개처럼 느껴지잖아요. 그래서 are를 쓰고 싶어지는 거예요. 하지만 영어 news는 항상 단수예요.
알렉스|en|In English, news works like water. You cannot say one news, two news, three news. It is not countable that way. So it always takes is.
민지|en|Think about it this way: Is there any water? Is there any news? Same pattern. The word takes is.
알렉스|en|The rule of thumb: if you cannot hold it in your hand and count it one by one, use is. News, water, information, advice. All say is.
민지|en|Let us practice that. Repeat after us: Is there any good news?
알렉스|en|Is there any good news?
민지|en|Good. Now: Is there any information about this?
알렉스|en|Is there any information about this? Nice.
민지|en|One more: Is there any advice you can give me?
알렉스|en|Is there any advice you can give me? You are getting it.
민지|en|Now the second big pattern. Some sentences this week were missing a verb. The action word just disappeared.
알렉스|en|Joey said: In company we usually online community board.
민지|en|Something feels wrong there, right? Like a word fell out.
알렉스|en|Exactly. The natural version is: We usually check the online community board at work. The verb check was missing.
민지|ko|한국어는 문맥이 있으면 동사를 생략해도 자연스러울 때가 있어요. 하지만 영어는 항상 동사가 있어야 해요. 말하기 전에 뭘 하는 거지를 먼저 떠올려 보세요.
알렉스|en|Simple trick: before you speak, ask yourself silently, what is the action? Check, use, access, post, pick up, call. Find that action word and put it in first.
민지|en|Let us try another one. Joey said: That is no make money.
알렉스|en|What is the action word here? Make. And what is the structure? That does not make money.
민지|en|No becomes does not. That is a very useful chunk. Does not, plus the plain dictionary word.
알렉스|en|Does not make. Does not pay. Does not work. Does not help. Practice that chunk.
민지|en|Say it with me: Volunteer work does not pay.
알렉스|en|Volunteer work does not pay. Good.
민지|en|One more verb pattern. Children.
알렉스|en|Joey said childrens twice this week. And it is completely understandable. Most English plurals just add S, right?
민지|en|Cat becomes cats. Dog becomes dogs. Friend becomes friends.
알렉스|en|But child becomes children. No S. It is an old, irregular form from Old English. Like tooth becomes teeth, or foot becomes feet.
민지|ko|child의 복수는 children이에요. 규칙 복수처럼 s를 붙이면 안 되고, children이 그 자체로 하나의 단어예요. 외워두면 평생 써요.
민지|en|Let us say it a few times: children, children, children.
알렉스|en|Children. I love working with children. Teaching children is enriching.
민지|en|Teaching children is enriching. Great sentence to remember.
알렉스|en|Okay, let us move to the Correction Clinic. Quick-fire corrections. Repeat after us if you can.
민지|en|You said: I am putting and gathering trash in riverside.
알렉스|en|Better: I am picking up trash by the riverside.
민지|en|Two changes: picking up instead of putting and gathering, and by the riverside instead of in riverside.
알렉스|en|Pick up trash. That is the action. And we say by the river, by the road, by the building. By, not in.
민지|en|You said: Maybe some email. Yeah, I have experience to read that.
알렉스|en|Better: Yeah, I think I have seen it in some emails. The chunk to know: I have seen it. That shows something that happened at some point before now.
민지|en|I have seen. I have heard. I have tried. These chunks say: at some point in my experience, this happened.
알렉스|en|You said: First, I am just called the control office in apartment.
민지|en|Better: I would just call the management office. Two things changed: I would instead of I am, and management office instead of control office.
알렉스|en|When you are talking about what you would do in a situation, say I would. I would call. I would check. I would just leave.
민지|en|I would ask my neighbor first. I would look it up online. I would wait and see. Great everyday phrase.
알렉스|en|And I would not. I would not do that. I would not say that. Very natural for expressing choices.
민지|en|You said: It is so hard to deal the trash.
알렉스|en|Better: Dealing with trash is such a hassle. The important fix: deal with, not deal. These two words travel together always.
민지|en|Deal with a problem. Deal with the situation. Deal with it. The with is part of the package.
알렉스|en|You said: I think my communication style is the middle, the indirectly and or blunt.
민지|en|Better: I would say my style is somewhere in the middle. Not too indirect, not too blunt.
알렉스|en|Somewhere in the middle is a great everyday expression. It works for personality, opinions, weather, anything.
민지|en|You said: It is same Asia, but if they are speaking, they do not speak directly.
알렉스|en|Better: We are both in Asia, but Japanese people tend not to say things directly.
민지|en|Tend not to. That is a softer, more natural way to describe a general habit. People tend to do this. People tend not to do that.
알렉스|en|You said: My sister coming into town that day, rain check?
민지|en|Better: My sister is coming into town that day. Can I take a rain check?
알렉스|en|Add is. My sister is coming. And rain check is a great phrase. It means: I cannot make it now, but can we reschedule?
알렉스|en|You said: I fell asleep at 10 and during almost 10 o clock, I sleep, good sleep.
민지|en|Better: I fell asleep at 10 and slept for about 9 or 10 hours. I slept really well.
알렉스|en|Two fixes: slept instead of sleep, and for about instead of during almost. Slept for about nine hours.
민지|en|You said: Oh, no. It is possible to continue.
알렉스|en|Better: No, I am good. Let us keep going. That is the casual, natural way to say you want to continue.

알렉스|en|This week the big word is community board. It is a shared notice board in a building, office, or neighborhood.

민지|en|Now, Culture Corner. This is my favorite part. Did you know?
알렉스|en|This week we keep coming back to the word news. Here is a fun fact. Some people say the word news is an acronym: North, East, West, South. Meaning information from all directions.
민지|ko|news가 북동서남 방위의 첫 글자를 딴 약자라는 이야기가 있어요. 사실 역사적으로 맞는 이야기는 아니지만, 뭔가 외우기에는 딱이죠.
알렉스|en|Most historians actually say this story is a folk tale. The word news in Old English just meant new things, new information.
민지|en|But I love the idea that news comes from every direction. North, east, west, south.
알렉스|en|And it makes the grammar point easy to remember. All that news from all those directions is still one thing. One big thing. So: is there any news?
민지|en|Here is the broader lesson. English has a group of words that look like they should be plural, but they always use is and never get an S. News, information, advice, furniture, luggage.
알렉스|en|You cannot say one advice or two furnitures. These words just are what they are, one whole thing.

알렉스|en|If you are ever unsure, just ask yourself: can I say one of them or two of them? One news? No. Two informations? No. Then use is.
