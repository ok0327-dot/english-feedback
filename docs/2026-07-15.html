💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


1.

❌ As Said: "I had my own pet just five days, six days."
✅ Natural: "I had a pet for about five or six days."
💎 Professional: "My experience owning a pet lasted only around five or six days."
💡 코칭 포인트: In English, a duration always needs "for" before the time period. "Just five days" works in casual speech but "for about five or six days" sounds much more natural. / 기간 앞에는 반드시 "for" 를 씁니다. "I had it for about five days."

2.

❌ As Said: "I got a pet on the road."
✅ Natural: "I found a puppy on the street."
💎 Professional: "I came across an abandoned puppy on the street."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Got a pet on the road" sounds like you bought it while traveling. "Found on the street" or "picked up off the street" clearly shows the puppy was a stray. / "on the road"는 여행 중에 구매한 것처럼 들려요. "found it on the street"이 자연스럽습니다.

3.

❌ As Said: "If somebody is a little sick, their character is a little changed."
✅ Natural: "When someone is sick, their personality changes a bit."
💎 Professional: "Illness often brings out a different side of people's personalities."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Character is changed" uses passive where English prefers active. Also "a little changed" sounds unnatural — use "changes a bit" or "shifts slightly." / 수동태보다 능동태가 자연스럽고, "a little changed" 대신 "changes a bit"를 씁니다.

📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)


1. separation anxiety /ˌsepəˈreɪʃən æŋˈzaɪ.ə.ti/ (noun phrase)
뜻: 분리 불안 (보호자가 없을 때 동물이나 아이가 극도로 불안해하는 상태)
실전 예문: "Our dog has separation anxiety, so we hired a dog walker."

2. adoption /əˈdɒp.ʃən/ (noun)
뜻: 입양 (버려진 동물을 공식 절차로 데려오는 것)

3. abandon /əˈbæn.dən/ (verb)
뜻: 버리다, 유기하다
실전 예문: "The puppy was abandoned by its previous owner."

4. stray /streɪ/ (adjective / noun)
뜻: 길 잃은 동물, 유기동물
실전 예문: "There are many stray cats in the neighborhood."

5. commitment /kəˈmɪt.mənt/ (noun)
뜻: 헌신, 지속적인 책임
실전 예문: "Owning a dog requires a long-term commitment of time and money."
