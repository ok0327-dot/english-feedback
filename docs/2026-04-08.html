💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "So if possible, yeah, I wanted to have free talking."

💡 코칭 포인트: "I wanted to" sounds like a past wish, but here you mean a present request, so "I'd like to" is more natural. ("I wanted to"는 과거의 바람처럼 들립니다. 지금 원하는 것을 정중히 요청할 때는 "I'd like to"가 자연스럽습니다.)

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "Did you hear of the menu, Hanjeongsik?"

💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "Are you familiar with a Korean course meal called Hanjeongsik?"
💡 코칭 포인트: We say "hear about / hear of," and the present perfect "Have you heard of...?" is the natural way to ask about prior knowledge. ("hear of/about"가 맞는 짝이고, 알고 있는지 물을 때는 현재완료 "Have you heard of...?"가 자연스럽습니다.)

3.

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "Every time, if I'm talking with you, free talking, at that time I'm a little nervous."

💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "I tend to feel a bit nervous whenever there's no set material to follow."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Whenever" already means "every time, when," so you can drop the extra "at that time." ("Whenever" 한 단어에 "매번, ~할 때"가 다 들어 있어 "at that time"을 빼면 훨씬 깔끔합니다.)
