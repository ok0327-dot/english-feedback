1. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "Yeah, it's getting more colder."

✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "Yeah, it's getting colder."

💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "Indeed, the temperature is noticeably decreasing."

💡 코칭 포인트: "When forming comparative adjectives, 'colder' is already the comparative form of 'cold'. We don't need 'more' before it. For most one-syllable adjectives and two-syllable adjectives ending in -y, we add -er. For longer adjectives, we use 'more'."

→ 비교급 형용사를 만들 때, 'colder'는 이미 'cold'의 비교급 형태입니다. 그 앞에 'more'를 붙일 필요가 없어요. 대부분의 한 음절 형용사나 -y로 끝나는 두 음절 형용사에는 '-er'을 붙이고, 더 긴 형용사에는 'more'를 사용합니다.


2. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "And when I prepare the present for my wife, the earring or some gold product, that product usually have a warranty."

✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "And when I buy presents for my wife, like earrings or other gold products, they usually come with a warranty."

💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "When purchasing gifts for my wife, such as jewelry or other valuable items, a warranty is typically included."

💡 코칭 포인트: "Using 'buy presents' is more common and natural than 'prepare the present'. Also, 'come with a warranty' is a very idiomatic and natural way to say a product includes a warranty. It highlights the idea that the warranty is an accompanying feature."

→ 'prepare the present'보다는 'buy presents'가 더 흔하고 자연스러운 표현입니다. 또한, 제품에 보증이 포함되어 있다는 것을 말할 때 'come with a warranty'라고 하면 훨씬 원어민스럽고 자연스럽습니다. 보증이 제품의 부속 기능이라는 뉘앙스를 강조해 줍니다.


3. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "The customer bought a vacuum cleaner, but two weeks later, the vacuum cleaner doesn't work anymore. So she wanted to need a refund."

✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "The customer bought a vacuum cleaner, but it stopped working after two weeks, so she wanted a refund."

💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "The customer purchased a vacuum cleaner, which ceased to function within two weeks, prompting her request for a refund."

💡 코칭 포인트: "Using 'it stopped working' is more concise and natural than 'doesn't work anymore' when talking about a past event. Also, 'wanted a refund' is the correct and common phrasing. 'Wanted to need a refund' is redundant and grammatically awkward."

→ 과거에 발생한 일에 대해 말할 때는 'doesn't work anymore'보다 'it stopped working'이 더 간결하고 자연스럽습니다. 또한, '환불을 원했다'는 'wanted a refund'가 정확하고 흔한 표현입니다. 'wanted to need a refund'는 중복되고 문법적으로 어색합니다.
