2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


① 영어의 장점 말하기

❌ As Said: "I think the language skill is possible to travel anywhere."
✅ Natural: "I think English makes it possible to travel anywhere."
💎 Professional: "Being proficient in English allows me to travel virtually anywhere."
💡 코칭 포인트: "A skill" can't *be* possible. It *makes* something possible. Use the chunk "A makes it possible to ~."

→ '실력'이 가능한 게 아니라, 실력이 무언가를 가능하게 만들어 주는 거예요. "A makes it possible to + 하다"를 한 덩어리로 외워 두세요.


② 더 나은 공부법이 필요하다고 말하기

❌ As Said: "So, I need to more way, I need to find more way how to speak better or more remember and make a sentence."
✅ Natural: "I need to find better ways to speak more naturally and remember what I learn."
💎 Professional: "I need to find more effective ways to build my speaking skills and retain what I've learned."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Find better ways to ~" is one smooth chunk. You don't need "how" after "ways."

→ "find better ways to + 하다"를 통째로 쓰면 깔끔해요. ways 뒤에 how를 또 붙이지 않아도 돼요. 그리고 "more remember"보다 "remember more"가 자연스러워요.


③ 실력이 제자리라는 느낌 말하기

❌ As Said: "sometimes my ability of English is stable, not a good achievement"
✅ Natural: "Sometimes I feel like I've hit a plateau."
💎 Professional: "Lately, my progress in English seems to have plateaued."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Stable" sounds positive, like "safe and steady." To say you're stuck, native speakers use "hit a plateau."

→ "stable"은 원어민 귀에 '안정적이라 좋다'로 들려요. '늘지 않고 멈춰 있다'는 뜻이라면 "hit a plateau"가 딱 맞아요. 오늘 Joey가 가장 하고 싶었던 말이죠!


④ 영어 공부가 스트레스는 아니라고 말하기

❌ As Said: "So my, I don't have a stress learning English"
✅ Natural: "Learning English doesn't stress me out."
💎 Professional: "I don't find learning English stressful at all."
💡 코칭 포인트: Stress isn't something you "have a" of. Say "~ stresses me out" or "I'm stressed about ~."

→ stress는 하나, 둘 세는 물건이 아니라서 "a stress"라고 하면 어색하게 들려요. "~ stresses me out(~때문에 스트레스 받아)" / "I'm stressed about ~"를 덩어리로 기억하세요.


⑤ 짝꿍 단어 빠뜨리지 않기

❌ As Said: "how to communicate to my teacher and how to use the sentence and listen your sentence"
✅ Natural: "how to talk with my teacher, use sentences, and listen to what you say"
💎 Professional: "how to communicate with my teacher, put sentences together, and follow what you're saying"
💡 코칭 포인트: Some words always travel with a buddy: *listen to*, *communicate with*, *travel to*.

→ 몇몇 단어는 늘 짝꿍과 함께 다녀요. listen to you, communicate with my teacher, travel to Japan. 오늘 "if I travel Japan"처럼 짝꿍이 빠지면 말이 중간에 툭 끊긴 것처럼 들려요. 짝꿍까지 한 덩어리로 입에 붙여 두세요.



3. 🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)


Category: verb tense


🎯 오늘의 패턴: 이미 지나간 일(오늘 아침, 어젯밤)을 지금 모양(get, is, go)으로 말하는 습관


Joey는 "today morning", "last night"처럼 시간 말은 잘 붙여 놓고도 동사는 지금 모양으로 말했어요. 원어민 귀엔 "어젯밤에… 추워요"처럼 시간이 엇갈려 들립니다.


❌ 원문 (As Said)✅ 교정 (Corrected)
"because last night it is so cold""because it was so cold last night"
"So today morning I get up early and just go to the gym.""This morning I got up early and went to the gym."
"I can't prepare my textbook because today morning I get up early…""I couldn't prepare for the lesson because I got up early this morning…"
"I think this day, you and me, we just have a free talking twice.""I think we've had free talk twice so far."

📌 핵심 규칙: 입에서 "last night / this morning / yesterday"가 나오면 동사도 지나간 모양으로 바꿔요. get → got, go → went, is → was, can't → couldn't. "지금까지 몇 번 해 왔다"는 have + 해 본 모양 덩어리(we've had, I've done)로 말합니다.

(덤 팁: "today morning"이 아니라 "this morning"이에요!)


🔁 따라 읽기

🗣️ "This morning I had breakfast and went to work early."

🗣️ "Yesterday I practiced English for 20 minutes."

🗣️ "We've had free talk twice, and I really liked it."

"but"으로 바람과 현실을 자연스럽게 이었어요. 칭찬과 아쉬움을 한 문장에 담아서 튜터의 마음을 움직였죠.

스케줄이 꽉 찼다는 말은 "difficult"보다 "pretty full"이 자연스러워요.
