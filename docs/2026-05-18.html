1. recall /rɪˈkɔːl/ (명사)

* 뜻: (제품의) 리콜, 회수

* 실전 예문: Due to a safety concern, the company issued a recall for its latest product line.


2. compromise /ˈkɒmprəmaɪz/ (동사/명사)

* 실전 예문: We need to find a compromise that satisfies both the client's demands and our budget constraints.


3. rough draft /rʌf drɑːft/ (명사)

* 뜻: 초고, 대략적인 원고

* 실전 예문: Please submit a rough draft of the proposal by Friday so we can review it.


4. constraint /kənˈstreɪnt/ (명사)

* 뜻: 제약, 제한

* 실전 예문: We are operating under tight budget constraints, so every expenditure must be justified.


5. mitigate /ˈmɪtɪɡeɪt/ (동사)

* 뜻: 완화시키다, 경감하다

* 실전 예문: Our goal is to mitigate the risks associated with the new market entry.
