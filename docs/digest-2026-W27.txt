## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "But usually in Seoul, the common transportation is subway because it is connected like a web, a spider web."
   Natural: "But in Seoul, the most common transportation is the subway, as it's interconnected like a spider web."
2. I said: "So usually I love to take the bus better than the subway."
   Natural: "So I usually prefer taking the bus over the subway."
3. I said: "I think the first I consider that work is about company or my work. I divide first."
   Natural: "I first consider whether the appointment is for the company or for myself. I'll divide them first."
4. I said: "There are four kinds of transportation or walk and divide the country."
   Natural: "There are four kinds of transportation, or people just walk, depending on the area."
5. I said: "So if you travel, first you take the transportation like the bus, subway, etc. And then we arrive at the endpoint, the popular place. We just walk."
   Natural: "So when you travel, you usually take public transportation like a bus or subway, and then once you arrive at your destination, you often walk."
6. I said: "almost driving is the error and that is my mistake because some busy area is so it is impossible to find the parking area."
   Natural: "Almost every time I've driven in a busy area, it's been a mistake because finding parking is impossible."

## Key vocabulary
- interconnected (adjective) — 서로 연결된, 상호 관련된
- predominant (adjective) — 우세한, 지배적인, 주요한
- idiomatic (adjective) — 관용적인, 숙어의
- pertains to (verb phrase) — ~에 관한 것이다, ~에 속하다
- consequently (adverb) — 결과적으로, 따라서
- utilize (동사) — 활용하다, 이용하다 (use보다 격식 있는 표현)
- congested (형용사) — 혼잡한, 붐비는
- impracticality (명사) — 비실용성, 비현실성
- oversight (명사) — 간과, 실수, 부주의
- hidden gem (명사) — 숨겨진 보석, 잘 알려지지 않은 좋은 곳/것
