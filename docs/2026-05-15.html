2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


Joey가 실제 말한 문장 중, 의미는 통하지만 더 자연스럽게 다듬을 수 있는 표현 3가지를 선정했습니다.


1. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "I will meet my family. So nice."

* ✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "I'm going to meet my family. That'll be nice."

* 💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "I'm looking forward to spending time with my family this weekend."

* 💡 코칭 포인트: "I will meet" is grammatically correct but "I'm going to meet" sounds more natural for expressing future plans, especially when it's a casual, pre-arranged activity. Adding "That'll be nice" makes the sentence flow better by expressing your feeling about the plan.

→ "I will meet"도 문법적으로 맞지만, "I'm going to meet"는 캐주얼하고 미리 정해진 계획을 말할 때 더 자연스럽습니다. "That'll be nice"를 덧붙이면 계획에 대한 Joey의 감정을 표현하여 문장이 더 유연하게 이어집니다.


2. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "the festival name is the sand festival near the beach."

* ✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "The festival is called the sand festival, and it's near the beach."

* 💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "The event is officially known as the sand festival, located by the beach."

* 💡 코칭 포인트: While "the festival name is" is understandable, "is called" is a much more common and natural way to introduce the name of an event or thing.

→ "the festival name is"도 이해는 되지만, "is called"는 행사나 사물의 이름을 소개할 때 훨씬 더 흔하고 자연스러운 표현입니다.


3. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "I think it is important to the area."

* ✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "I think it depends on the area."

* 💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "The relevance of this point is largely contingent upon the specific region."

* 💡 코칭 포인트: "Important to the area" doesn't quite convey the meaning of "it varies by area." "Depends on" is the correct and natural phrase to express that something is conditional or variable based on a factor.

→ "Important to the area"는 '지역에 따라 다르다'는 의미를 정확히 전달하지 못합니다. 어떤 것이 특정 요인에 따라 조건적이거나 가변적임을 표현할 때는 "Depends on"이 정확하고 자연스러운 표현입니다.

1. tackle /ˈtækl/ (동사)

* 뜻: (문제 등을) 다루다, 해결하려고 노력하다

* 실전 예문: We need to tackle this budget deficit before it gets out of hand.


2. contingent upon /kənˈtɪndʒənt əˈpɒn/ (형용사구)

* 뜻: ~에 달려있는, ~을 조건으로 하는

* 실전 예문: Our investment decision is contingent upon the market's performance next quarter.


3. protocol /ˈproʊtəkɔːl/ (명사)

* 뜻: (외교적) 의례, 의전; (컴퓨터) 통신 규약; (일반적인) 규칙, 절차

* 실전 예문: Please follow the established protocol for submitting expense reports.


4. nuance /ˈnjuːɑːns/ (명사)

* 뜻: 미묘한 차이, 뉘앙스

* 실전 예문: Understanding the cultural nuances is crucial for effective international negotiation.


5. facilitate /fəˈsɪlɪteɪt/ (동사)

* 뜻: (과정 등을) 용이하게 하다, 촉진하다
