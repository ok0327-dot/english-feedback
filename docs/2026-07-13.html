1. ❌ As Said: "That is enjoyable for my life."

✅ Natural: "It's enjoyable for me."

💎 Professional: "I find it very fulfilling."

💡 코칭 포인트: Using "enjoyable for my life" is understandable, but "enjoyable for me" is more direct and natural. "Fulfilling" is a great word for something that brings deep satisfaction.

→ "enjoyable for my life"는 의미 전달은 되지만, "enjoyable for me"가 훨씬 직접적이고 자연스러운 표현입니다. 좀 더 격식 있는 자리에서는 "fulfilling"을 사용하여 깊은 만족감을 표현할 수 있습니다.


2. ❌ As Said: "So if the subject is changed at the time, the verb also changes, right?"

✅ Natural: "So if the subject changes, the verb also changes, right?"

💎 Professional: "Consequently, a shift in the subject necessitates a corresponding change in the verb conjugation, correct?"

💡 코칭 포인트: The passive voice "is changed" is often unnecessary when the subject performs the action. "At the time" is also a bit redundant here.

→ 주어가 직접 행동하는 경우, 수동태 "is changed"는 불필요할 때가 많습니다. 또한, "at the time"도 이 문맥에서는 다소 반복적인 표현입니다.
