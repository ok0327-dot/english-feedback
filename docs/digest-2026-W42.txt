## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "Mexico is only summer, but it also has a winter time."
   Natural: "I thought Mexico was always summer, but it also has a winter season."
2. I said: "Usually I forbid them from gathering my cookies because my cookies are there, they use that and then they give us some biased information."
   Natural: "I usually prevent them from collecting my cookies because they use that data to provide biased information."
3. I said: "I carefully to the outside because usually I take a bicycle."
   Natural: "I was careful going outside because I usually ride my bicycle."
4. I said: "Just today I go walk."
   Natural: "Today I just walked."
5. I said: "if I meet some difficult things, I need to refresh to go outside or just take a break to the work."
   Natural: "If I encounter difficulties, I need to refresh myself by going outside or just take a break from work."
6. I said: "Yeah, it's a good time for doing work."
   Natural: "Yeah, it's a good time to get work done."
7. I said: "Just I'm wondering that."
   Natural: "I was just wondering about that."
8. I said: "Emma wanted to drop her at the library, and she took the bus."
   Natural: "Emma wanted to be dropped off at the library, so she took the bus."

- respite (명사) — 짧은 휴식, 일시적인 중단
