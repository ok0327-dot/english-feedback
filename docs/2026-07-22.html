1. ❌ As Said: "This day, I have something to do at night, so I slept at a really late time, so I'm really tired, but it's okay."

✅ Natural: "I had something to do last night, so I went to bed really late, and I'm quite tired today, but it's okay."

💡 코칭 포인트: Using "last night" clarifies that the late sleep happened in the past, causing current tiredness. "Went to bed really late" is a more common and natural way to express sleeping late. "This day" is not a common phrase for "today".

'This day'는 '오늘'을 의미할 때 잘 쓰지 않는 표현이며, 'slept at a really late time'보다는 'went to bed really late'가 훨씬 자연스러운 구어체입니다. 'last night'을 써서 어젯밤에 늦게 잤다는 것을 명확히 해주는 것이 좋습니다.

💡 코칭 포인트: "The more it's a normal day" is grammatically understandable but sounds a bit clunky. "On weekdays" is a much more direct and common way to refer to normal working days. "Every person" can be replaced with "most people" for a more natural flow.

'the more it's a normal day'는 어색하게 들릴 수 있습니다. '평일'을 의미할 때는 간단히 'On weekdays'라고 하는 것이 가장 자연스럽습니다. 'every person' 대신 'most people'을 사용하면 더 유연한 표현이 됩니다.
