### 2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)

Joey가 말한 문장 중 의미는 통하지만, 원어민이 들었을 때 더 자연스럽게 들릴 수 있는 표현들을 살펴볼게요.


1. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "His ability is so great."

* ✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "He's very capable." or "He's highly skilled."

* 💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "He demonstrates exceptional capability." or "His performance has been outstanding."

* 💡 코칭 포인트: Using "capable" or "skilled" as adjectives directly describes a person's competence more naturally than using the noun "ability" with "great." "Great" is often too general.

→ "'capable'이나 'skilled'와 같은 형용사를 사용하면, 'great'이라는 일반적인 형용사와 명사 'ability'를 사용하는 것보다 사람의 역량을 더 자연스럽게 묘사할 수 있습니다."


2. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "I'm always welcome to your meeting."

* ✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "I'm always happy to meet with you." or "I'm always available for your meetings."

* 💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "I'm always pleased to attend your meetings." or "I'm always happy to make myself available for your meetings."

* 💡 코칭 포인트: "Welcome to" is typically used when you are *receiving* someone (e.g., "Welcome to my office"). When you are the one attending or accepting an invitation, it's more natural to say you are "happy to meet" or "available."

→ "'Welcome to'는 보통 누군가를 *환영할 때* 사용됩니다 (예: 'Welcome to my office'). 회의에 참석하거나 초대를 수락하는 입장일 때는 'happy to meet' 또는 'available'이라고 표현하는 것이 더 자연스럽습니다."


3. ❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "This software is, usually the investor always gathering information, the stock market, real estate, and bond, etc."

* ✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "This software helps investors gather information from the stock market, real estate, and bond markets."

* 💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "This software streamlines the information gathering process for investors, covering areas such as the stock market, real estate, and bonds."

* 💡 코칭 포인트: The original sentence was a bit fragmented. By restructuring it to clearly state what the software *does* for investors, the sentence becomes much clearer and more concise.

→ "원문은 다소 단편적이었습니다. 소프트웨어가 투자자들을 위해 *무엇을 하는지* 명확하게 재구성하면 문장이 훨씬 명확하고 간결해집니다."
