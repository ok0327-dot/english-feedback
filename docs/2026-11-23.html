2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


① 날씨 이야기

❌ As Said: "Oh, there is a cold. It is hard to get up early, yeah, because outside is cold and it's getting there, going down temperature, yeah."
✅ Natural: "It's so cold! It's hard to get up early because it's freezing outside, and the temperature keeps dropping."
💎 Professional: "It's been quite cold lately. The temperature has been dropping steadily, so getting up early has been a challenge."
💡 코칭 포인트: "There is a cold" sounds like "someone has a cold." For weather, just say "It's cold." Also, "The temperature keeps dropping" is one natural chunk.

→ "There is a cold"는 원어민 귀에 "감기가 있다"처럼 들려요. 날씨는 그냥 It's cold. 로 충분해요. "기온이 계속 떨어져요"는 The temperature keeps dropping. 을 한 덩어리로 외워 두세요.


② 오늘 일정 설명

❌ As Said: "Just every lunch time I go for walk near park. So except that situation, maybe I work inside in my office."
✅ Natural: "I go for a walk in the park every lunchtime. Other than that, I'll be working in my office."
💎 Professional: "Aside from my lunchtime walk, I'll be in the office for the rest of the day."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Except that situation" sounds stiff and textbook-like. Natives say "Other than that" or "Aside from that." It's short, smooth, and super common. Also, "go for a walk" always comes with "a."

→ "except that situation"은 딱딱하게 들려요. 원어민은 Other than that, ~ (그거 말고는요)를 훨씬 많이 써요. "산책 가다"는 a를 넣은 go for a walk 를 통째로 입에 붙이세요. "the rest of the day"(나머지 하루 동안)도 같이 챙겨 두세요.


③ 나이 들면 머리가 하얘지는 이유

❌ As Said: "Okay, when we get older, get it, get old orders, our melanin pigment is lower, so our hair become to change to white."
✅ Natural: "As we get older, our bodies make less melanin, so our hair turns white."
💎 Professional: "As we age, melanin production decreases, which is why our hair turns gray or white."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Become to change to white" stacks three words that all mean "change." Just say "turns white." It's one short chunk. And "make less melanin" sounds more natural than "melanin is lower."

→ "become to change to white"는 '바뀌다'를 세 번 겹쳐 말한 셈이에요. "하얘지다"는 turns white 한 덩어리면 끝나요(turns gray, turns red도 같은 모양). "멜라닌이 덜 만들어져요"는 make less melanin 으로 말해 보세요.


④ 기사에 대한 의견

❌ As Said: "I think this article is quite long time ago because these days the culture is a little changed in Asia."
✅ Natural: "I think this article is a bit outdated. The culture in Asia has changed a little these days."
💎 Professional: "This article seems somewhat dated. Attitudes in Asia have shifted quite a bit in recent years."
💡 코칭 포인트: An article can't "be a long time ago." Instead, it's "outdated" or "dated." One word says it all. And "has changed" shows the change is still true now.

→ 기사가 "오래 전"일 수는 없어요. "좀 옛날 얘기 같다"는 a bit outdated 한 단어로 끝나요. "바뀌어서 지금도 그 상태"라는 느낌은 has changed 덩어리로 말해요.



3. 🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)


Category: verb patterns (want + noun vs. want to + verb)


🎯 오늘의 패턴: "~을 원해"는 want + 그것, "~하고 싶어"는 want to + 하다예요. fair는 '하다'가 아니라서 want to 뒤에 바로 붙일 수 없어요.


Joey는 오늘 "하얀 피부를 원한다"를 말할 때마다 want to + fair라고 했어요. to 뒤에는 go, have, get, look처럼 '무언가를 하는 말'이 와야 해요. fair(하얀)는 모습을 나타내는 말이라, to 뒤에 오면 소리가 툭 끊겨 어색하게 들려요.


❌ 원문 (As Said)✅ 교정 (Corrected)
"Okay, many East Asia, East South Asia people want to fair skin."Many East and Southeast Asian people want fair skin.
"So they want to fair skin."So they want to have fair skin.
"Many people want to fair face."Many people want a fair complexion.

📌 핵심 규칙: 갖고 싶은 '것'이면 want + 그것 (want fair skin)이에요. '하고 싶은 일'이면 want to + 하는 말 (want to have / want to get / want to look)이에요. 헷갈리면 want to have를 통째로 쓰면 안전해요!


🔁 따라 읽기

🗣️ "Many Asian people want fair skin."
🗣️ "So they want to have fair skin."
🗣️ "Many people want to look brighter."


4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)


① epitome /ɪˈpɪtəmi/ (noun)

뜻: 전형, 완벽한 본보기 (발음은 '에피톰'이 아니라 '이피터미'예요. 끝에 '-미' 소리가 살아 있어요)
덩어리: the epitome of ~ (~의 완벽한 본보기)
실전 예문: *Our CFO is the epitome of a careful investor.*

② quintessential /ˌkwɪntɪˈsenʃəl/ (adjective)

뜻: 가장 전형적인, 딱 ~다운
실전 예문: *This is a quintessential example of poor internal control.*

③ pigment /ˈpɪɡmənt/ (noun)

뜻: 색을 내는 성분, 색소 (옷을 염색하는 물감, 피부색을 정하는 멜라닌 같은 것)
실전 예문: *Melanin is the pigment that gives our skin and hair their color.*

④ mainstream /ˈmeɪnstriːm/ (adjective/noun)

뜻: 주류의, 대세의
덩어리: It's mainstream (in ~) for people to + 하다 (~에서는 사람들이 …하는 게 대세예요)
실전 예문: *LNG has become a mainstream energy source in Korea.*

⑤ hefty /ˈhefti/ (adjective)

뜻: (가격·금액이) 꽤 큰, 묵직한
덩어리: a hefty price / a hefty penalty
실전 예문: *The company had to pay a hefty penalty after the audit.*
