## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "a little exciting and a little bit upset"
   Natural: "a little excited and a little bit upset"
2. I said: "we can't generalize about hobbies per country."
   Natural: "we can't generalize hobbies by country."
3. I said: "every place is possible to play the singing song."
   Natural: "I can sing songs anywhere."
4. I said: "How about my opinion?"
   Natural: "How about my suggestion?"
5. I said: "it is more adapt to outdoor activity to summer"
   Natural: "Summer is more suitable for outdoor activities."
6. I said: "just I find some close CVS or Olive Young and then just buying them"
   Natural: "I'd just find a nearby CVS or Olive Young and buy some."
7. I said: "I want to make you happy."
   Natural: "I want to be helpful."
8. I said: "When I met some people, I feel awkward."
   Natural: "When I meet new people, I sometimes feel awkward."
9. I said: "These days in Korea, so many young generation, like the MG generation, they exchange their social media account..."
   Natural: "These days in Korea, many young people, like the MZ generation, exchange social media accounts..."
10. I said: "I will go back to my home and it's possible to meet my family."
   Natural: "I'll go home and see my family."
11. I said: "So, some people like to eating out, go to the visiting a cafe, and some people like to going to concert or activities."
   Natural: "So, some people like eating out or visiting cafes, while others prefer going to concerts or doing outdoor activities."
12. I said: "We find some new restaurant and new street and new cafe. We find a place and walk and found some new place."
   Natural: "We find new restaurants, new streets, and new cafes. We find a place, walk around, and discover new spots."

## Key vocabulary
- apprehensive (형용사) — 걱정하는, 염려하는
- infrastructure (명사) — 사회 기반 시설 (도로, 통신망 등)
- versatility (명사) — 다용도성, 다재다능함
- intensive (형용사) — 집중적인, 많은 노력을 요하는
- volatility (명사) — 변동성, 불안정성
- terrain (명사) — 지형, 지역
- conducive (형용사) — ~에 좋은, 도움이 되는
- procure (동사) — (어렵게) 얻다, 조달하다
- deteriorate (동사) — 악화되다, 나빠지다
- resilience (명사) — 회복력, 탄력성
- approachable (adjective) — 가까이하기 쉬운, 친근한
- common ground (noun phrase) — 공통점, 공통의 관심사
- awkward (adjective) — 어색한, 서투른
- exchange (verb) — 교환하다
- polite (adjective) — 예의 바른, 공손한
- No-show (noun) — 약속 장소에 나타나지 않는 사람
- Back out (phrasal verb) — 약속이나 계획을 취소하다
- it by ear (idiom) — 즉흥적으로 상황에 맞춰 결정하다, 미리 계획하지 않고 그때그때 봐가며 하다
- Couch potato (noun) — (주로 주말에) 소파에 앉아 텔레비전만 보며 시간을 보내는 사람, 게으름뱅이
- Impulsive (adjective) — 충동적인
