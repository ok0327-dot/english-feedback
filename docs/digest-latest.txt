## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "Yeah, it's getting more colder."
   Natural: "Yeah, it's getting colder."
2. I said: "And when I prepare the present for my wife, the earring or some gold product, that product usually have a warranty."
   Natural: "And when I buy presents for my wife, like earrings or other gold products, they usually come with a warranty."
3. I said: "The customer bought a vacuum cleaner, but two weeks later, the vacuum cleaner doesn't work anymore. So she wanted to need a refund."
   Natural: "The customer bought a vacuum cleaner, but it stopped working after two weeks, so she wanted a refund."
4. I said: "I don't have experience visit the Asian market abroad"
   Natural: "I haven't had the chance to visit an Asian market abroad."
5. I said: "usually if I travel to abroad, I just focus on their country."
   Natural: "Usually, when I travel abroad, I prefer to focus on experiencing the local culture."
6. I said: "If you want something spicy, try put in gochujang."
   Natural: "If you want something spicy, try putting in gochujang."
7. I said: "But sometimes they instruction to the little weak ."
   Natural: "But sometimes the instructions are a bit unclear ."
8. I said: "Just to do many laundry . That is more easy for me anyway."
   Natural: "It's just easier for me to do a larger load of laundry ."
9. I said: "If any has a stain, I ask them to treat with a gentle detergent."
   Natural: "If anything has a stain, I ask them to treat it with a gentle detergent."
10. I said: "This day is awesome season."
   Natural: "It's an awesome season today."
11. I said: "So spring and autumn season is short in Korea. So, if we don't enjoy that season, it passes fast."
   Natural: "Spring and autumn are short in Korea. So, if we don't enjoy those seasons, they pass quickly."
12. I said: "And I'm just middle-aged in life."
   Natural: "I'm middle-aged."
13. I said: "Because she works too late, so she wanted the non-drowsy medicine."
   Natural: "Because she will be working later, she needed the non-drowsy medicine."

## Key vocabulary
- eligible (형용사) — ~을 가질 자격이 있는, 적격의
- defective (형용사) — 결함이 있는, 불량의
- malfunctioning (형용사) — 오작동하는, 기능 불량의
- unresponsive (형용사) — 반응이 없는, 응답하지 않는
- extended warranty (명사구) — 연장 보증 (기본 보증 기간 이후 추가로 제공되는 보증)
- crave (동사) — ~을 갈망하다, 간절히 바라다
- authentic (형용사) — 진짜의, 진정한, 정통의
- exotic (형용사) — 이국적인, 색다른
- immerse (동사) — 몰두하다, 푹 빠지다 (주로 'immerse oneself in' 형태로 사용)
- assimilate (동사) — 동화되다, 흡수하다
- detergent (명사) — 세제 (세탁용)
- delicate (형용사) — 섬세한, 약한 (옷감 등이)
- stain (명사) — 얼룩
- duvet (명사) — (솜이나 털을 넣어 만든) 이불, 덮개
- coin-operated (형용사) — 동전으로 작동되는
- audit (n, v) — (회계) 감사; 감사하다
- drowsiness (n) — 졸음, 나른함
- symptoms (n) — (병의) 증상
- hygienic (adj) — 위생적인
