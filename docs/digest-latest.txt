## Recurring mistakes from earlier weeks — my long-term habits
My most repeated weak categories overall: Articles (a/an/the), Verb Tense. These corrections are from earlier lessons in those categories. Please weave a few into the review so I revisit old mistakes.
1. (2026-10-07, Articles (a/an/the)) I said: "Because another room alarm woke me up. So I can't sleep after the alarm."
   Natural: "Another alarm from a different room woke me up, so I couldn't go back to sleep."
2. (2026-10-07, Articles (a/an/the)) I said: "These days I have been eyeing the Mac Mini, an Apple product, because these days it is possible to turn on always in my home and possible to remote control AI."
   Natural: "These days, I've been eyeing the Mac Mini, an Apple product, because it can always be on at home and I can remotely control AI with it."
3. (2026-10-07, Articles (a/an/the)) I said: "I need to figure out a little more. And I wait for the sale periods for this product, for this company."
   Natural: "I need to do a bit more research. I'm also waiting for this product to go on sale."
4. (2026-09-22, Articles (a/an/the)) I said: "Today's topic is what you go to drink, right?"
   Natural: "Today's topic is about 'go-to' drinks, right?"
5. (2026-09-22, Articles (a/an/the)) I said: "I was wondering about 'go-to' is the usual expression?"
   Natural: "I was wondering if 'go-to' is a commonly used expression?"
6. (2026-09-22, Articles (a/an/the)) I said: "Because that is about to respect the mind."
   Natural: "Because it's a sign of respect."
7. (2026-09-21, Verb Tense) I said: "Maybe how about your country's beauty?"
   Natural: "What about the autumn in your country?"
8. (2026-09-21, Verb Tense) I said: "my bag is old, almost three years more past"
   Natural: "my bag is old, it's been over three years."
9. (2026-09-21, Verb Tense) I said: "I can't win through my desire."
   Natural: "I can't resist my cravings."
10. (2026-09-16, Articles (a/an/the)) I said: "After work, I will go to some dinner with my friends in Yeouido Park."

- technical difficulties (명사구) — 기술적인 문제, 기술적인 어려움
