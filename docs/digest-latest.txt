## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "So the temperature is still high, but it's getting a little lower, going low."
   Natural: "So the temperature is still high, but it's starting to cool down a bit."
2. I said: "Yeah, that's normal, but it's so special for me. It's so nice."
   Natural: "It was a simple outing, but it felt very special to me."
3. I said: "So if I want, I will go to a different beach, because we have different beaches."
   Natural: "So I can visit a different beach whenever I want, because there are many."
4. I said: "And you have a happy Monday night."
   Natural: "I hope you had a happy Monday night."
5. I said: "Where is popular for a first date in your country?"
   Natural: "What is a popular place for a first date in your country?"
6. I said: "talking something."
   Natural: "talking about something."
7. I said: "my printed paper is really removed some document."
   Natural: "Some parts of my printed paper are missing."
8. I said: "usually the summertime I had so sweat on my body but nowadays it's more fresh and there is no sweat."
   Natural: "Usually in the summertime I would sweat a lot, but nowadays it's much fresher and I don't sweat."
9. I said: "I love to Middle Asia airline."
   Natural: "I love Middle Eastern airlines."
10. I said: "Just the short word is named vocabulary."
   Natural: "Vocab is just the short form of vocabulary."
11. I said: "Almost I've used the same backpack for ten years."
   Natural: "I've used the same backpack for almost ten years."
12. I said: "I'm prepared for next week in China."
   Natural: "I'm getting ready for my trip to China next week."
