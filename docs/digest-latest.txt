## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "Oh, there is a cold. It is hard to get up early, yeah, because outside is cold and it's getting there, going down temperature, yeah."
   Natural: "It's so cold! It's hard to get up early because it's freezing outside, and the temperature keeps dropping."
2. I said: "Just every lunch time I go for walk near park. So except that situation, maybe I work inside in my office."
   Natural: "I go for a walk in the park every lunchtime. Other than that, I'll be working in my office."
3. I said: "Okay, when we get older, get it, get old orders, our melanin pigment is lower, so our hair become to change to white."
   Natural: "As we get older, our bodies make less melanin, so our hair turns white."
4. I said: "I think this article is quite long time ago because these days the culture is a little changed in Asia."

6. I said: "So, I need to more way, I need to find more way how to speak better or more remember and make a sentence."

10. I said: "So I want the new style textbook, select the new style textbook, and just go, yeah, that textbook, use that textbook."

## Key vocabulary
- epitome (noun) — 전형, 완벽한 본보기 (발음은 '에피톰'이 아니라 '이피터미'예요. 끝에 '-미' 소리가 살아 있어요)
- quintessential (adjective) — 가장 전형적인, 딱 ~다운
- pigment (noun) — 색을 내는 성분, 색소 (옷을 염색하는 물감, 피부색을 정하는 멜라닌 같은 것)
- mainstream (adjective/noun) — 주류의, 대세의
- hefty (adjective) — (가격·금액이) 꽤 큰, 묵직한
- plateau (noun / verb) — 정체기, 제자리걸음 (하다)
- carve out (verb phrase) — 바쁜 와중에 시간을 따로 떼어 내다
- pencil in (verb phrase) — (일정을) 임시로 잡아 두다
- language barrier (noun) — 언어 장벽
- lifelong learner (noun) — 평생 배우는 사람
- suburban (adjective) — 교외의, 도심에서 떨어진 주거지역의 (반대말: urban = 도심의)
- snore (verb) — 코를 골다 (코 고는 사람 = a snorer)
- gesture (noun) — (마음을 표현하는) 행동, 성의 표시
- complaint (noun) — 불만 제기, 항의 (make a complaint = 항의하다)
- mild (adjective) — (날씨가) 온화한, 포근한
