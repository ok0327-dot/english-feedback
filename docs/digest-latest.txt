## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "Today. Yes, it's a tired Monday because last night, on Sunday, I had ice swimming with my son."
   Natural: "It's a tiring Monday because last night, on Sunday, I went ice swimming with my son."
2. I said: "Is it more going down ?"
   Natural: "Is it getting colder ?"
3. I said: "I always saw the the Terracotta about history book ."
   Natural: "I always read about the Terracotta Warriors in history books ."
4. I said: "Why that expression used the element?"
   Natural: "Why is that expression used with 'element'?"
5. I said: "Yes, usually if I make a plan to travel , I'm just mixed , relaxation and adventure."
   Natural: "Yes, usually if I plan a trip , I like to mix relaxation and adventure."
6. I said: "I think this sentence meaning is a change in environment or a change in situation."
   Natural: "I think this phrase means a change in environment or situation."
7. I said: "Every word that includes 'took,' is it possible to also use 'take,' 'take a turn'?"
   Natural: "For phrases like 'took a turn,' can I also use 'take a turn'?"
8. I said: "I had three luggages."
   Natural: "I had three pieces of luggage."
9. I said: "After work, I will go to some dinner with my friends in Yeouido Park."
   Natural: "After work, I will have dinner with my friends in Yeouido Park."
10. I said: "Everything is perfect for the weather. We have a vivid color and tree."
   Natural: "Everything is perfect for this weather. We have vivid colors and trees ."
11. I said: "I think that writer has such a great imagination and made a story."
   Natural: "I think that writer has such a great imagination and created a compelling story ."

## Key vocabulary
- ultimate (형용사) — 궁극적인, 최종적인, 최고의
- destination (명사) — 목적지, 행선지
- element (명사) — 요소, 성분; (in one's element) 자기에게 가장 편안하고 잘 맞는 환경
- inspire (동사) — 영감을 주다, 고무하다, 격려하다
- tranquil (형용사) — 고요한, 평온한
- itchy feet (idiom) — 여행하고 싶어 안달이 난 마음, 떠나고 싶은 마음
- take a turn (phrase) — 상황이 바뀌다, 전환점을 맞다 (주로 예상치 못한 방향으로)
- turn things around (phrase) — 상황을 역전시키다, 개선하다 (나쁜 상황을 좋은 상황으로)
- accommodation (n) — 숙박 시설
- backup plan (n) — 비상 계획, 대안
- hype (명사) — 과대광고, 과장된 선전
- miss the mark (숙어) — 목표를 달성하지 못하다, 기대에 미치지 못하다
- thought-provoking (형용사) — 생각할 거리를 주는, 시사하는 바가 많은
- unresolved (형용사) — 미해결의, 결론이 나지 않은
- prowess (명사) — 뛰어난 능력, 기량
