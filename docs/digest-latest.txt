## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "I have to interview at dinner time, so yeah, so I need to change my schedule."
   Natural: "I have an interview scheduled for dinner time, so I need to change my schedule."
2. I said: "After work, if we have an interview, at that time it is kept secret to the investor or research."
   Natural: "If we conduct an interview after work, it's kept confidential from investors or for research purposes."
3. I said: "Do you have an experience to take an ambulance or a helicopter?"
   Natural: "Have you ever taken an ambulance or a helicopter?"

## Key vocabulary
- audit (명사, 동사) — 회계 감사, 심사; (장부 등을) 감사하다, 심사하다
- confidential (형용사) — 기밀의, 비밀의
- reschedule (동사) — 일정을 변경하다, 다시 잡다
- utilize (동사) — 활용하다, 이용하다 (use보다 격식 있는 표현)
- responsive (형용사) — 즉각 반응하는, 반응이 빠른; (요구에) 부응하는
