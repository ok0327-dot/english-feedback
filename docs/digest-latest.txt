## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "It's a Monday and it's going into autumn season , so it's a little chilly."
   Natural: "It's Monday, and as autumn approaches , it's a little chilly."
2. I said: "I also knew about Canada and USA. That is the, that code is the 911."
   Natural: "I also know about Canada and the USA; their emergency number is 911."
3. I said: "What is the big big emergency situation, emergency scare from the natural?"
   Natural: "What's the biggest natural emergency that scares you?"
4. I said: "So if we go outside, a little chilly, windy."
   Natural: "It's a little chilly and windy if you go outside."
5. I said: "But it is limited, only for personal work."
   Natural: "But it's limited to personal use."
6. I said: "That is my meaning."
   Natural: "That's what I mean."
7. I said: "Today, we have a dinner with my coworker."
   Natural: "Today, I'm having dinner with my coworker."
8. I said: "I think I'm just half."
   Natural: "I think it's fifty-fifty."
9. I said: "I'm so easy a person and some people might think he is so easy a person, so I always get walked all over."
   Natural: "I'm such an easygoing person that some people might take advantage of me."
10. I said: "And what is the stuff? Stuff is the same meaning as belonging?"
   Natural: "What is 'stuff'? Is 'stuff' the same as 'belongings'?"
11. I said: "And our company also sometimes uses the passcode because it is so simple, but it is also that security is so strong."
   Natural: "Our company also sometimes uses a passcode because it's simple yet very secure."
12. I said: "First, I confirm my lost item. If I mistake or some step, move just a minute, a second."
   Natural: "First, I'd confirm that my item is actually lost, in case I just misplaced it or stepped away for a moment."

## Key vocabulary
- onset (n) — 시작, 발병
- utilize (v) — 활용하다, 사용하다 (use보다 격식 있는 표현)
- scenario (n) — (예상되는) 시나리오, 상황
- contingency (n) — 만일의 사태, 비상사태
- prone (adj) — ~하기 쉬운, ~의 경향이 있는
- clarify (동사) — 명확하게 하다, 분명히 하다
- thread (명사) — (온라인 대화의) 스레드, 연속된 메시지

- perseverance (명사) — 인내, 끈기
- colleague (명사) — (직장) 동료
