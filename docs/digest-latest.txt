## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "Yeah, it's okay, but I need to help to change my textbook. Is it possible to help?"
   Natural: "Could you help me change my textbook?"
2. I said: "That is permanently finished or just finished temporarily, the textbook?"
   Natural: "Is the textbook gone for good, or is it just temporary?"
3. I said: "I changed my showerhead because before the change, my showerhead leaked the small water, and that water power is so weak."
   Natural: "I replaced my showerhead because the old one was leaking a little, and the water pressure was really weak."
4. I said: "What's mean the patching up?"
   Natural: "What does 'patching up' mean?"
5. I said: "Many employees had a distress during the work, during the task, during their task, due to their task."
   Natural: "A lot of employees are stressed out because of their workload."
6. I said: "That is impossible to get sleep in the night."
   Natural: "It's when you can't fall asleep at night."
7. I said: "I'm almost understanding this paragraph. So yeah, I have no question."
   Natural: "I understand most of this paragraph, so I don't have any questions."
8. I said: "Ten years later, I supposedly become rich."
   Natural: "Ten years from now, I'll hopefully be rich."
9. I said: "Sometimes I turn on the YouTube and do yoga with my family. … sometimes I can listen the birds singing."
   Natural: "Sometimes I put on a yoga video on YouTube and do it with my family. With the windows open, I can hear the birds singing."
10. I said: "Today, it's a normal Wednesday, the middle of week. So, maybe it is, I have some routine of my normal days."
   Natural: "It's just a regular Wednesday, the middle of the week. I'm sticking to my usual routine today."
11. I said: "I think if I have a headache or my eye was sick, sore my eyes, and this signal is that I had so many stress."
   Natural: "When I get a headache or my eyes feel sore, that's a sign I'm under a lot of stress."
12. I said: "So just I release my stress to yoga, to yoga in the weekends, or to swimming in a hotel pool, or walk the near park."
   Natural: "I usually relieve stress by doing yoga on weekends, swimming in a hotel pool, or taking a walk in a nearby park."
13. I said: "Is there some different pronunciation in the word?"
   Natural: "Did I mispronounce any of the words?"
14. I said: "Yeah, it is a little chilly, but everything's beautiful and my condition is also great."
   Natural: "It's a little chilly, but it's a beautiful day, and I'm feeling great too."
15. I said: "Some guy passed the exams to use the AI. I heard."
   Natural: "I heard someone used AI to cheat on the exam."
16. I said: "And every morning, I on the scale and check my weight."
   Natural: "Every morning, I step on the scale and check my weight."
17. I said: "The team leader usually have accountable to the task."
   Natural: "The team leader is usually accountable for the task."

## Key vocabulary
- finishing touch (noun phrase) — 마무리 손질, 최종 마무리 (보통 "put / add the finishing touches to / on ~" 덩어리로 써요)
- patch up (phrasal verb) — (임시로) 고치다, 땜질하다. 다툰 사이를 수습한다는 뜻도 있어요.
- mend (verb) — 수선하다, 고치다
- water pressure (noun) — 수압
- stepladder (noun) — 접이식 사다리 (Joey가 "stepper"라고 찾던 그 단어예요)
- distress (noun) — 극심한 걱정·괴로움, 고통
- relieve (verb) — (스트레스·고통을) 덜어 주다, 풀어 주다
- manifest (verb) — (증상·문제가) 드러나다, 나타나다
- supposedly (adverb) — 소문에 의하면, ~라고들 하던데 (확실하지 않다는 느낌)
- eustress (noun) — 긍정적인 스트레스 (나를 성장시키는 좋은 긴장감)
- perception (noun) — 인식, 사람들이 무언가를 바라보는 시각
- stimulate (verb) — 자극하다, 활성화하다
- metabolism (noun) — 신진대사, 음식을 에너지로 바꾸는 몸의 과정
- equipped (adjective) — (능력이나 장비를) 갖춘
- accountable (adjective) — 책임이 있는, 결과를 설명할 의무가 있는
- consistency (noun) — 꾸준함, 일관성
- cookie-cutter (adjective) — 똑같이 찍어낸 듯한, 개성 없는
