2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


Expression 1
❌ As Said: "I will go back to my home and it's possible to meet my family."
✅ Natural: "I'll go home and see my family."
💎 Professional: "I plan to return home and spend time with my family."
💡 Coaching Point: "Go home" is a very common and natural phrasing. Adding "my" before "home" is usually unnecessary when referring to your own residence. Also, "it's possible to meet" sounds a bit formal and indirect for casual plans. "See my family" or "meet my family" is more direct and natural.

→ 'Go home'은 매우 흔하고 자연스러운 표현입니다. 자신의 집을 말할 때 'home' 앞에 'my'를 붙이는 것은 보통 불필요합니다. 또한, 캐주얼한 계획에 대해 'it's possible to meet'는 다소 격식 있고 간접적으로 들립니다. 'See my family' 또는 'meet my family'가 더 직접적이고 자연스럽습니다.


Expression 2
❌ As Said: "So, some people like to eating out, go to the visiting a cafe, and some people like to going to concert or activities."
✅ Natural: "So, some people like eating out or visiting cafes, while others prefer going to concerts or doing outdoor activities."
💎 Professional: "Consequently, certain individuals enjoy dining out or frequenting cafes, whereas others opt for attending concerts or engaging in outdoor pursuits."
💡 Coaching Point: After verbs like "like," it's more natural to use gerunds (verb + -ing) like "eating out" and "visiting" or infinitives like "to eat out" and "to visit." Saying "go to the visiting a cafe" is grammatically incorrect. Also, "activities" is often used in plural form when referring to a general category of things to do.

→ 'Like'과 같은 동사 뒤에는 'eating out'이나 'visiting'과 같은 동명사(verb + -ing) 또는 'to eat out', 'to visit'와 같은 부정사를 사용하는 것이 더 자연스럽습니다. "go to the visiting a cafe"는 문법적으로 틀렸습니다. 또한, 'activities'는 일반적으로 할 수 있는 일들의 범주를 말할 때 복수형으로 자주 사용됩니다.


Expression 3
❌ As Said: "We find some new restaurant and new street and new cafe. We find a place and walk and found some new place."
✅ Natural: "We find new restaurants, new streets, and new cafes. We find a place, walk around, and discover new spots."
💎 Professional: "We discover new dining establishments, thoroughfares, and coffee shops. We locate an area, explore it, and uncover novel locations."
💡 Coaching Point: When talking about general types of places, using the plural form (restaurants, streets, cafes) is often more natural. Also, ensure verb tense consistency. You used "find" and "walk" (present tense) and then "found" (past tense) in close succession. Using "discover" or "find" for the third instance maintains consistency. "New place" can be pluralized to "new places" or "new spots" for variety.

→ 일반적인 종류의 장소에 대해 말할 때는 복수형(restaurants, streets, cafes)을 사용하는 것이 더 자연스러운 경우가 많습니다. 또한, 동사 시제를 일관성 있게 유지하는 것이 중요합니다. "find"와 "walk"(현재 시제)를 사용한 후 바로 "found"(과거 시제)를 사용했습니다. 세 번째 경우에 "discover" 또는 "find"를 사용하여 일관성을 유지했습니다. "New place"는 다양성을 위해 "new places" 또는 "new spots"로 복수형으로 바꿀 수 있습니다.

4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)


1. No-show /noʊ ˈʃoʊ/ (noun)
뜻: 약속 장소에 나타나지 않는 사람
실전 예문: "We had a few no-shows for the client meeting, which was unexpected."

2. Back out /bæk ˈaʊt/ (phrasal verb)
뜻: 약속이나 계획을 취소하다
실전 예문: "He had to back out of the project due to a family emergency."

3. Play it by ear /pleɪ ɪt baɪ ˈɪr/ (idiom)
뜻: 즉흥적으로 상황에 맞춰 결정하다, 미리 계획하지 않고 그때그때 봐가며 하다
실전 예문: "We don't have a strict agenda for the team outing; we'll play it by ear."

4. Couch potato /ˈkaʊtʃ pəˌteɪtoʊ/ (noun)
뜻: (주로 주말에) 소파에 앉아 텔레비전만 보며 시간을 보내는 사람, 게으름뱅이
실전 예문: "After a long week, I sometimes feel like a couch potato on Saturday mornings."

5. Impulsive /ɪmˈpʌlsɪv/ (adjective)
뜻: 충동적인
실전 예문: "My manager is very organized, unlike my impulsive nature when planning trips."
