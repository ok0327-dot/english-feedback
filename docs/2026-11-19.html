2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


① 컨디션 표현

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "Yeah, it is a little chilly, but everything's beautiful and my condition is also great."
✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "It's a little chilly, but it's a beautiful day, and I'm feeling great too."
💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "It's a bit chilly, but it's a lovely day, and I'm feeling energized and ready to go."
💡 코칭 포인트:

Native speakers rarely say "my condition is great." Talking about your 'condition' sounds medical, like a doctor's report. Use "I'm feeling ~" to talk about how you feel today.

→ 원어민은 "my condition is great"라고 거의 말하지 않아요. 'condition'이라고 하면 병원 진료 기록처럼 들리거든요. 오늘 몸 상태를 말할 때는 "I'm feeling ~"을 한 덩어리로 쓰세요. (I'm feeling great / I'm feeling a bit tired)


② 뉴스 전하기

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "Some guy passed the exams to use the AI. I heard."
✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "I heard someone used AI to cheat on the exam."
💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "According to the news, a test-taker was caught using AI during the exam."
💡 코칭 포인트:

Start with "I heard ~" so the listener knows it's news right away. Also, "passed the exams to use AI" sounds like the goal of passing was to use AI. "Used AI to cheat" puts the action first and the purpose after.

→ "I heard ~"로 문장을 시작하면 듣는 사람이 바로 '아, 들은 소식이구나' 하고 알아들어요. 또 "passed the exams to use AI"라고 하면 'AI를 쓰려고 시험에 붙었다'처럼 거꾸로 들려요. "used AI to cheat"처럼 한 일을 먼저 말하고, 목적은 "to + 하다"로 뒤에 붙이세요.


③ 체중계에 올라가기

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "And every morning, I on the scale and check my weight."
✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "Every morning, I step on the scale and check my weight."
💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "I weigh myself every morning, and the data syncs automatically to my app."
💡 코칭 포인트:

The action word is missing here. "Step on the scale" is the natural chunk native speakers use. "Weigh myself" is even shorter and cleaner.

→ 지금 문장에는 '무엇을 하는지'에 해당하는 말이 빠져 있어서, 원어민 귀엔 말이 중간에 툭 끊긴 것처럼 들려요. "step on the scale"(체중계에 올라가다)을 한 덩어리로 외워 두세요. 더 짧게 "weigh myself"(몸무게를 재다)라고 해도 깔끔합니다.


④ 책임이 있다고 말하기

❌ 학습자 원문 (As Said): "The team leader usually have accountable to the task."
✅ 자연스러운 표현 (Natural): "The team leader is usually accountable for the task."
💎 비즈니스 표현 (Professional): "The team leader is ultimately accountable for the project's outcome."
💡 코칭 포인트:

"Accountable" describes what someone *is*, so it goes with "is," not "have." Remember the chunk "be accountable for + the task," and "be accountable to + a person" (like "accountable to the voters").

→ accountable은 '책임이 있는 상태'를 말하는 단어라 have가 아니라 is와 짝이에요. 맡은 일에는 "be accountable for + 일", 보고해야 하는 사람에게는 "be accountable to + 사람" 덩어리로 외워 두세요. 그리고 주인공이 '팀장 한 사람'이면 have가 아니라 is·has 쪽이 붙어요. (He is accountable for… / She has a lot of work.)



3. 🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)


Category: quantifiers (almost vs. most / almost all)


🎯 오늘의 패턴: '거의 다·대부분'을 말할 때 almost 하나만 덜렁 쓰는 습관


Joey는 '대부분'이라는 뜻으로 almost를 혼자 쓰는 경우가 많았어요. 하지만 almost는 혼자서 '대부분'이 되지 못해요. 늘 all, every, 숫자 같은 말 앞에 붙어서 '거의 ~'를 만드는 꼬리표 같은 말이에요.


❌ 원문 (As Said)✅ 교정 예시
"Yeah, almost many people go for work at 10 a.m. because it is an important day.""Most people go to work at 10 a.m. because it's an important day."
"...because almost the house is similar in Asia.""...because almost all the houses look similar in Asia."
"And I have that data almost during four years.""I've had that data for almost four years."
"Almost. But metabolism is a little difficult for me." (단어를 대부분 안다는 답변)"I know most of them, but metabolism is a little difficult for me."

📌 핵심 규칙: '대부분'은 most, '거의 다'는 almost all / almost every, '거의 4년 동안'은 for almost four years. almost는 꼭 all, every, 숫자 같은 말과 붙여서 한 덩어리로 쓰세요.


🔁 따라 읽기:

🗣️ "Most people go to work late on exam day."
🗣️ "Almost all the houses in my neighborhood look the same."
🗣️ "Almost every morning, I go for a run."
