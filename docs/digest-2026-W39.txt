## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "Maybe how about your country's beauty?"
   Natural: "What about the autumn in your country?"
2. I said: "my bag is old, almost three years more past"
   Natural: "my bag is old, it's been over three years."
3. I said: "I can't win through my desire."
   Natural: "I can't resist my cravings."
4. I said: "Today's topic is what you go to drink, right?"
   Natural: "Today's topic is about 'go-to' drinks, right?"
5. I said: "I was wondering about 'go-to' is the usual expression?"
   Natural: "I was wondering if 'go-to' is a commonly used expression?"
6. I said: "Because that is about to respect the mind."
   Natural: "Because it's a sign of respect."

   Natural: "I think that writer has such a great imagination and created a compelling story ."

## Key vocabulary
- spontaneously (부사) — 즉흥적으로, 자발적으로
- craving (명사) — 갈망, 열망 (특히 음식에 대한 강한 욕구)
- curated (형용사) — 엄선된, 큐레이션 된
- ambiance (명사) — 분위기 (특히 장소의 분위기)
- culinary (형용사) — 요리의, 음식의
- go-to (형용사) — (어떤 상황에서) 믿고 의지하는, 즐겨 찾는, 단골의
- hooked on (형용사구) — ~에 푹 빠진, ~에 중독된
- lightweight (명사) — (술에) 약한 사람, (권투 등에서) 경량급 선수
- heavyweight (명사) — (술에) 강한 사람, (권투 등에서) 중량급 선수; 영향력 있는 사람
- sober (형용사) — 술 취하지 않은, 맨정신의; 진지한, 냉철한
