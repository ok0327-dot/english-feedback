2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


① 프리토킹 요청하기

❌ As Said: "So is it possible to just pre-talking?"
✅ Natural: "Could we just do some free talking today?"
💎 Professional: "Would it be alright if we just had a free conversation today instead?"
💡 코칭 포인트: "Free talking" is a Korean-style phrase, and "pre-talking" sounds like "talking before something." Native speakers usually say "just chat" or "have a free conversation." Starting with "Could we…" makes a request sound soft and polite.

→ 'Free talking'은 한국식 표현이고, 'pre-talking'은 '미리 하는 대화'처럼 들립니다. 원어민은 보통 "just chat"이나 "have a free conversation"이라고 해요. 부탁할 때 "Could we…"로 시작하면 부드럽고 공손하게 들립니다.


② 루틴이 일정하다고 말하기

❌ As Said: "Oh yeah, it's similar because I'm salary man, so my work time is correct, so usually my routine is also same"
✅ Natural: "My routine is pretty much the same every day because I work regular hours at a company."
💎 Professional: "Since I work fixed hours as a corporate employee, my daily routine stays fairly consistent."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Salaryman" isn't really used in English (your tutor even asked about it!), so "employee" or "office worker" is clearer. "My work time is correct" sounds like the time is "right or wrong." Say "regular hours" or "fixed hours" instead. "Pretty much the same every day" is an easy chunk to keep on your tongue.

→ 'Salaryman'은 영어권에서 잘 안 쓰는 말이에요. 튜터도 무슨 뜻이냐고 되물었죠! "employee"나 "office worker"라고 하면 더 분명합니다. "My work time is correct"는 시간이 '맞다/틀리다'라는 말처럼 들려요. 대신 "regular hours"나 "fixed hours"라고 하면 됩니다. "pretty much the same every day"는 통째로 입에 붙여두기 좋은 덩어리예요.


③ 만드는 건 쉬운데, 쓰는 게 어렵다

❌ As Said: "But usually it is easy to make some program, but it is difficult to listen to radio or read my homepage and read my email."
✅ Natural: "Building the program is the easy part. Actually listening to it and reading the emails is the hard part."
💎 Professional: "Developing the tool is straightforward; the real challenge is using it consistently."
💡 코칭 포인트: Using "but" twice in one sentence makes the contrast blurry. The chunk "~ is the easy part, ~ is the hard part" makes your point sharp and natural. Adding "actually" stresses the part that really matters.

→ 한 문장에 "but"이 두 번 나오면 무엇과 무엇을 비교하는지 흐려집니다. "~ is the easy part, ~ is the hard part" 덩어리를 쓰면 하고 싶은 말이 딱 살아나요. "actually"를 넣으면 '실제로 쓰는 게' 어렵다는 느낌이 강조됩니다.


④ 두 회사 비교하기

❌ As Said: "So if I compare two companies, this SK is more have a well system. So every and people is the more smart, smarter."
✅ Natural: "Compared to my old company, SK has better systems, and the people are really smart."
💎 Professional: "Compared to my previous employer, SK has much more established systems, and my colleagues are very capable."
💡 코칭 포인트: To say "더 좋은," just use one word: "better." No need for "more" or "well." Short words get "-er" at the end (smart → smarter). "Compared to ~" is a handy chunk for starting any comparison.

→ '더 좋은'은 "better" 한 단어면 끝이에요. "more"나 "well"을 붙일 필요가 없어요. smart처럼 짧은 말은 끝에 -er만 붙이면 됩니다(smart → smarter). 비교를 시작할 때는 "Compared to ~"를 통째로 쓰면 편해요. 앞서 Joey가 직접 "smarter"로 고쳐 말한 것도 아주 좋았어요!



3. 🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)


Category: verb tense

🎯 오늘의 패턴: '어제 일 / 요즘 늘 하는 일 / ~한 지 몇 년째'에 따라 동사 모양을 바꿔주기


Joey는 오늘 어제 일은 지금 모양으로, 요즘 늘 하는 일은 지난 일 모양으로 거꾸로 말하는 경우가 많았어요. 그러면 원어민은 "언제 일이야?" 하고 시간이 헷갈립니다.


❌ 원문 (As Said)✅ 교정 (Corrected)시간 신호
"Yesterday, my eye is so sick."Yesterday, my eyes were really sore.yesterday → was/were
"Because I don't prepare my textbook today."I didn't prepare my textbook today.이미 지나간 준비 → didn't
"Yeah, so usually, this day I studied AI, use my personal computer."These days, I study AI on my personal computer.these days → study
"So, yeah, so usually I made some program for me"Usually, I make programs for myself.usually → make
"Maybe 11 years, 12 years I work here."I've worked here for about 11 or 12 years.~한 지 N년 → I've worked … for

📌 핵심 규칙: 말하기 전에 문장 속 시간 단어를 먼저 보세요.

yesterday / last week → 지난 일 모양 (was, did, made)
usually / these days / every day → 사전에 나오는 모양 그대로 (study, make, ride)
"~한 지 N년째" → I've + done + for N years를 한 덩어리로

🔁 따라 읽기

🗣️ "Yesterday, my eyes were really sore."

🗣️ "These days, I study AI on my personal computer."

🗣️ "I've worked here for about 11 or 12 years."
