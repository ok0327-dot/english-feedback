1. 📊 오늘의 대화 요약 (Summary)

새 교재 일정을 정했습니다. 화요일은 Power Verb & Collocation, 목요일과 금요일은 Daily Topic English로 진행합니다.
Daily Topic의 "Cultural Understanding: Meeting Styles"를 다뤘습니다. 회의 사진을 묘사했고, 회사의 회의 방식(대면·화상·전화), 감사팀에서 회의를 주관한 경험, 회사 회의 문화(formal vs. casual), 미국 제약회사를 감사할 때 겪은 문화 차이를 이야기했습니다.

2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


① 회의 방식이 상황에 따라 달라진다고 말할 때

❌ As Said: "So it is different to what kind of meeting and purpose and attending member etc."
✅ Natural: "So it depends on the type of meeting, the purpose, and who's attending."
💎 Professional: "The format varies depending on the type of meeting, its objective, and the participants."
💡 코칭 포인트: "It depends on ~" is the go-to chunk native speakers use when something changes case by case. "Who's attending" sounds much smoother than "attending member."

→ "상황마다 달라요"는 "It depends on ~" 한 덩어리로 말하면 됩니다. "attending member"보다 "who's attending(누가 오는지)"가 훨씬 매끄럽게 들려요.


② 직접 만나서 회의하기 어렵다고 말할 때

❌ As Said: "So sometimes it is impossible physical meeting."
✅ Natural: "So sometimes it's impossible to meet in person."
💎 Professional: "Meeting face-to-face isn't always feasible, so we often rely on video conferencing."
💡 코칭 포인트: Native speakers say "meet in person" or "face-to-face" far more often than "physical meeting." Also, "it's impossible to + action" works as one natural chunk.

→ 원어민은 "physical meeting"보다 "meet in person / face-to-face"를 훨씬 자주 씁니다. "~하는 게 불가능해요"는 "it's impossible to + 하다(meet, go처럼 사전 모양 그대로)"를 통째로 익혀 두세요.


③ 미국인의 소통 방식이 더 직설적이라고 말할 때

❌ As Said: "I think their communication way is more directly better than our culture."
✅ Natural: "I think they communicate much more directly than we do."
💎 Professional: "Their communication style is noticeably more direct than what we're used to in Korea."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Communication way" sounds unnatural. Say "communication style," or use the action itself: "they communicate." Also, "more directly better" stacks two comparisons, so pick just one.

→ "communication way"는 어색하게 들려요. "communication style"이라고 하거나 "they communicate(그들은 소통한다)"처럼 동작으로 말해 보세요. "more directly better"는 "더"를 두 번 겹친 셈이라 하나만 쓰면 됩니다.


④ 처음엔 알아듣기 힘들었다고 말할 때

❌ As Said: "And first, I can understand their communication." / "Because they language is so expert and it is a little fast and I can understand their perfect, so it is a little hard."
✅ Natural: "At first, I couldn't really understand them because they spoke fast and used a lot of technical terms."
💎 Professional: "Initially, I struggled to follow the discussion. They spoke quickly and used a lot of industry jargon."
💡 코칭 포인트: You meant "can't," but you said "can," which gives the opposite meaning. For a past story, "couldn't" is clearer and safer. Also, "at first" is the natural chunk for "처음에는."

→ Joey는 "알아듣기 어려웠다"는 뜻으로 말했지만, "I can understand"는 원어민 귀엔 "잘 알아들었어요"로 들려요. 뜻이 정반대가 됩니다! 지나간 일이니 "I couldn't understand"라고 하면 헷갈릴 일이 없어요. "처음에는"은 "first"가 아니라 "at first" 한 덩어리로 말하세요. "they language is so expert"는 "they used a lot of technical terms(전문 용어를 많이 썼다)"로 바꾸면 자연스럽습니다.



3. 🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)


Category: plural nouns (countable nouns)

🎯 오늘의 패턴: 여러 개를 말할 때 단어 끝의 s를 자꾸 빠뜨려요 (튜터도 마지막에 짚은 부분!)

"Yes, I give the, made some question and listen the answer.""I make some questions and listen to the answers."

📌 핵심 규칙: 앞에 some / many / two / six / kinds of가 붙으면 듣는 사람 귀는 "여러 개"를 기다려요. 그래서 끝에 s를 붙여야 말이 툭 끊기지 않고 자연스럽게 들립니다. (some questions, two kinds of meetings, six members) 몇 명이 왔는지 말할 때는 "There are + 숫자 + 사람들(-s)"를 한 덩어리로 외워 두세요.

4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)


1) chair /tʃer/ (verb)

뜻: (회의를) 주재하다, 진행하다
실전 예문: I chaired the kickoff meeting for the internal audit last week.

2) agenda /əˈdʒendə/ (noun)

뜻: 회의 안건, 의제
실전 예문: I'll send the agenda and the Teams link by tomorrow morning.

3) interviewee /ˌɪntərvjuːˈiː/ (noun)

뜻: 면담 대상자, 인터뷰를 받는 사람
실전 예문: I scheduled meetings with five interviewees from the finance team.

4) strategic /strəˈtiːdʒɪk/ (adjective)

뜻: 전략적인
실전 예문: We had a strategic planning meeting about next year's LNG imports.

5) variety /vəˈraɪəti/ (noun)

뜻: 다양함, 여러 종류 → "a wide variety of + 여러 개(-s)" 덩어리로 쓰세요
실전 예문: Our office has a wide variety of meeting rooms for different purposes.
