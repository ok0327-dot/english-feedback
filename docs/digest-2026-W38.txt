## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "Today. Yes, it's a tired Monday because last night, on Sunday, I had ice swimming with my son."
   Natural: "It's a tiring Monday because last night, on Sunday, I went ice swimming with my son."
2. I said: "Is it more going down ?"
   Natural: "Is it getting colder ?"
3. I said: "I always saw the the Terracotta about history book ."
   Natural: "I always read about the Terracotta Warriors in history books ."
4. I said: "Why that expression used the element?"
   Natural: "Why is that expression used with 'element'?"
5. I said: "Yes, usually if I make a plan to travel , I'm just mixed , relaxation and adventure."
   Natural: "Yes, usually if I plan a trip , I like to mix relaxation and adventure."
