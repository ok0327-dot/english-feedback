✅ Natural: "Tomorrow we're traveling."

💡 코칭 포인트: Using the present continuous for future plans (e.g., 'we're traveling') makes the sentence sound more immediate and natural for a confirmed plan. 'We will travel' is grammatically correct but can sound a bit more formal or like a spontaneous decision.

→ 현재 진행형을 미래의 확정된 계획에 사용하면 (예: 'we're traveling') 더 즉각적이고 자연스럽게 들립니다. 'We will travel'도 문법적으로 맞지만, 좀 더 격식 있거나 즉흥적인 결정처럼 들릴 수 있어요.


2. ❌ As Said: "I'm taking twice per day, two classes per day."

💎 Professional: "I'm currently taking two classes daily."
