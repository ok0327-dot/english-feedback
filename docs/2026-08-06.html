2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


1) ❌ As Said: "I want to make you happy."

✅ Natural: "I want to be helpful." / "I want to do a good job."

💎 Professional: "I aim to meet your expectations." / "I'm committed to ensuring your satisfaction."

💡 Coaching Point: "The original sentence 'I want to make you happy' is understandable but can sound a bit too personal or child-like in a professional context. Using 'helpful' or 'do a good job' focuses on your contribution, and 'meet your expectations' or 'ensure your satisfaction' are more professional ways to express your desire to please in a business setting."

→ 원문 "I want to make you happy"는 의미는 통하지만, 비즈니스 맥락에서는 다소 개인적이거나 어린아이처럼 들릴 수 있습니다. 'helpful'이나 'do a good job'을 사용하면 기여하는 부분에 초점을 맞출 수 있고, 'meet your expectations' 또는 'ensure your satisfaction'은 비즈니스 환경에서 상대방을 만족시키고자 하는 마음을 표현하는 더 전문적인 방법입니다.


2) ❌ As Said: "When I met some people, I feel awkward."

✅ Natural: "When I meet new people, I sometimes feel awkward."

💎 Professional: "I occasionally feel awkward when encountering new people."

💡 Coaching Point: "The original sentence uses 'met' which is past tense, but the feeling of awkwardness seems to be a general or recurring experience. Using the present tense 'meet' and adding 'sometimes' or 'occasionally' makes it clear this is a general feeling or habit rather than a specific past event. Also, 'new people' is slightly more common than 'some people' in this general context."

→ 원문은 과거 시제인 'met'를 사용했지만, 어색함을 느끼는 것은 일반적이거나 반복적인 경험처럼 들립니다. 현재 시제인 'meet'를 사용하고 'sometimes' 또는 'occasionally'를 추가하면 이것이 특정 과거 사건이 아닌 일반적인 감정이나 습관임을 명확히 할 수 있습니다. 또한, 이 일반적인 맥락에서는 'some people'보다 'new people'이 약간 더 흔하게 사용됩니다.


3) ❌ As Said: "These days in Korea, so many young generation, like the MG generation, they exchange their social media account..."

✅ Natural: "These days in Korea, many young people, like the MZ generation, exchange social media accounts..."

💎 Professional: "Currently, in Korea, a significant portion of the younger demographic, particularly the MZ generation, tends to exchange social media handles..."

💡 Coaching Point: "The phrase 'so many young generation' is a bit unnatural. It should be 'many young people' or 'a lot of young people'. Also, 'they exchange their social media account' can be simplified to 'exchange social media accounts' or 'exchange social media handles' for a smoother flow. The term 'MZ generation' is more common in Korean context than 'MG generation'."

→ 'so many young generation'이라는 표현은 다소 부자연스럽습니다. 'many young people' 또는 'a lot of young people'이 되어야 합니다. 또한, 'they exchange their social media account'는 더 자연스러운 흐름을 위해 'exchange social media accounts' 또는 'exchange social media handles'로 단순화할 수 있습니다. 'MG generation'보다는 한국 맥락에서 'MZ generation'이라는 용어가 더 일반적입니다.
