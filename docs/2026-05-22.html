2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)

1. ❌ 원문: "I make some plans with my family. What to do, etc."

✅ 자연스러운 표현: "I'm making plans with my family, like deciding what to do."

💎 비즈니스 표현: "I'm in the process of finalizing plans with my family, including activities and itineraries."

💡 코칭 포인트: 'I make' 대신 'I'm making'을 사용하여 현재 진행 중인 계획임을 나타내고, 'like'를 활용하여 자연스럽게 추가 설명을 덧붙일 수 있습니다.


2. ❌ 원문: "I will go to China and the city name is Xi'an."

✅ 자연스러운 표현: "I'm planning to visit Xi'an, China."

💎 비즈니스 표현: "I intend to travel to Xi'an, China, as part of my upcoming trip."

💡 코칭 포인트: 'I will go to ~ and the city name is ~' 대신 'I'm planning to visit ~'를 사용하여 계획을 더 간결하고 자연스럽게 표현할 수 있습니다.


3. ❌ 원문: "I heard that many European countries, but that is a more easy and public place to travel."

✅ 자연스러운 표현: "I've heard that many European countries are easy to travel to, but Spain is particularly accessible."

💎 비즈니스 표현: "From what I understand, numerous European countries are relatively easy to travel to, with Spain being a notable example."

💡 코칭 포인트: 'a more easy and public place' 대신 'particularly accessible'과 같이 더 자연스러운 형용사 표현을 사용하고, 문장 구조를 다듬어 유창성을 높일 수 있습니다.


3. 🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)

Category: Verb Tense Consistency & Natural Phrasing

❌ 원문: "I make two plans. One is the Chinese Xi'an. The second is Spain in wintertime."

✅ 교정 예시: "I've made two plans. One is to visit Xi'an, China. The second is to travel to Spain in the winter."

❌ 원문: "I will go to China, to Xi'an. That city is the oldest city in China. So maybe almost 3,000 years ago, they became the capital and stayed for a long time the capital in the oldest China."

✅ 교정 예시: "I'm planning to visit Xi'an, China, which is one of the oldest cities in China. It became the capital almost 3,000 years ago and remained so for a long time in ancient China."

❌ 원문: "So did you hear the... Just a moment, I'm searching. Hmm... Sorry. Qin Shi Huang, that's the first emperor of China."

✅ 교정 예시: "Have you heard of... (Just a moment, I'm searching)... Qin Shi Huang, the first emperor of China?"

📌 핵심 규칙: 시제를 일관성 있게 사용하고, 문장을 더 자연스럽고 간결하게 연결하는 연습이 필요합니다. 특히, 과거의 경험이나 사실을 말할 때는 현재완료 시제('I've made', 'I've heard')를, 현재의 계획을 말할 때는 현재 진행형('I'm planning to')을 활용하는 것이 좋습니다. 또한, 불필요한 반복을 줄이고 관계대명사 등을 활용하여 문장을 매끄럽게 연결해 보세요.

🔁 따라 읽기:

🗣️ "I've made two plans. One is to visit Xi'an, China. The second is to travel to Spain in the winter."

🗣️ "I'm planning to visit Xi'an, China, which is one of the oldest cities in China. It became the capital almost 3,000 years ago and remained so for a long time in ancient China."

🗣️ "Have you heard of Qin Shi Huang, the first emperor of China?"

1. Terracotta /ˌtɛrəˈkɒtə/ (명사)

뜻: 테라코타 (점토를 구워 만든 조각품이나 건축 재료)

실전 예문: "The Terracotta Warriors are a famous tourist attraction in Xi'an, China."


2. Voucher /ˈvaʊtʃər/ (명사)

뜻: 상품권, 할인권, 교환권

실전 예문: "The hotel gave us a voucher for our family to use during our stay."


3. Scampi /ˈskæmpi/ (명사)

뜻: 새우 스캠피 (마늘, 버터 등으로 요리한 새우 요리)

실전 예문: "I'd like to try the shrimp scampi at the Italian restaurant."


4. Olive oil /ˌɒlɪv ˈɔɪl/ (명사)

뜻: 올리브유

실전 예문: "Olive oil is a key ingredient in Mediterranean cuisine."


5. Tapas /ˈtæpəs/ (명사)

뜻: 타파스 (스페인에서 식사 전에 술과 곁들여 먹는 소량의 음식)

실전 예문: "Let's go to a tapas bar and try some small plates."


5. 📝 실전 복습 챌린지 (Practice)

1. 한국어 상황 설명: 당신은 스페인 여행을 계획하고 있습니다. 친구에게 여행 계획을 설명해 보세요.

영작 요구: "I'm planning to visit Spain next month. I've already booked my flights and accommodation."

모범 답안: "I'm planning to visit Spain next month. I've already booked my flights and accommodation. I'm really looking forward to trying all the delicious food and drinks."


2. 한국어 상황 설명: 당신은 중국 여행에서 만난 사람에게 여행 경험이 좋았는지 물어보는 질문을 받았습니다. 대답해 보세요.

영작 요구: "How was your trip to China?"

모범 답안: "It was amazing! I loved trying all the local foods and visiting the historical sites. The people were also very friendly and helpful."


3. 한국어 상황 설명: 당신은 스페인 여행에서 올리브 오일을 맛보는 경험을 이야기하고 싶습니다. 이야기해 보세요.

영작 요구: "I had a great experience trying olive oil in Spain."

모범 답안: "I had a great experience trying olive oil in Spain. We visited a local farm and learned about the process of making olive oil. We also got to taste some of the freshest olive oil I've ever had."
