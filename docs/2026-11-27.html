2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


① 경험이 없다고 말할 때

❌ As Said: "Yes, I don't have experience, but it's okay, it's possible to answer."
✅ Natural: "I've never had a pet like that, but that's okay. I can still talk about it."
💎 Professional: "I don't have much firsthand experience, but I'm happy to share my thoughts on it."
💡 코칭 포인트: "It's possible to answer" sounds like a rule or permission. "I can still talk about it" sounds personal and confident. "Firsthand experience" is a clean way to say "I didn't do it myself."

→ "It's possible to answer"는 규칙이나 허락처럼 들려요. "I can still talk about it"은 내 의지가 담겨 훨씬 자신감 있게 들립니다. "firsthand experience(직접 해 본 경험)"는 회의에서도 바로 쓸 수 있는 덩어리예요.


② 물고기를 키웠다고 말할 때

❌ As Said: "In my case, yeah, maybe 10 years ago, I take the fish."
✅ Natural: "About 10 years ago, I had a pet fish."
💎 Professional: "Around ten years ago, I kept a few fish in an aquarium at home."
💡 코칭 포인트: In English, we don't "take" a pet. We "have" or "keep" one. "Take" sounds like you grabbed the fish and walked away. Also, "10 years ago" needs the past form: had, kept.

→ 영어에서 반려동물은 take 하지 않고 have / keep 해요. "take the fish"는 원어민 귀엔 '물고기를 집어 들고 갔다'처럼 들립니다. 그리고 '10년 전' 이야기니까 had / kept처럼 '그때' 모양으로 말해야 해요. "키웠어요"는 통째로 I had a pet ~ / I kept ~ 로 기억하세요.


③ 옛날 이야기를 끝까지 '그때' 모양으로 말할 때

❌ As Said: "But one month later, when I came back to my home after school, that dog is gone somewhere."
✅ Natural: "But a month later, when I came home from school, the dog was gone."
💎 Professional: "About a month later, I came home from school and found that the dog was gone."
💡 코칭 포인트: You started the story in the past ("came back"), so keep it in the past: "was gone," not "is gone." "Is gone" suddenly jumps to right now. Also, say "come home," not "come back to my home."

→ "came"로 잘 시작했는데 중간에 "is gone"이 끼어서, 원어민 귀엔 갑자기 '지금' 이야기로 튀는 것처럼 들려요. 옛날 이야기는 처음부터 끝까지 came → was 처럼 '그때' 모양으로 쭉 이어 가세요. 오늘 같은 습관이 또 있었어요: "we bring that fish" → we brought. 그리고 home 앞에는 to를 안 붙여요. come home / go home 을 한 덩어리로 외우세요.


④ 대가족에게 개가 잘 맞는다고 말할 때

❌ As Said: "if some guy have a big family and live the suburbans, that is good chance to take care the dog"
✅ Natural: "If someone has a big family and lives in the suburbs, it's a good chance to take care of a dog."
💎 Professional: "For a large family living in the suburbs, a dog can be a great fit."
💡 코칭 포인트: "Take care of" always comes as one chunk. Don't drop "of." Use "live in the suburbs," and "someone" instead of "some guy," which sounds too casual. When the subject is one person, add s: someone has / lives.

→ "돌보다"는 항상 take care of 세 단어가 한 덩어리예요. of가 빠지면 말이 중간에 끊긴 것처럼 들려요. "교외에 살다"는 live in the suburbs 로 통째로 외우세요. "some guy"는 '어떤 녀석'처럼 너무 가볍게 들리니 someone이 좋아요. 그리고 주인공이 '누군가 한 사람'이면 동사 끝에 s를 붙여요: someone has / lives.


⑤ 질문해도 되는지 물어볼 때

❌ As Said: "Is it out of the question, or I need to change my question?"
✅ Natural: "Is that okay to ask, or should I ask something else?"
💎 Professional: "Is that an appropriate question, or would you prefer I rephrase it?"
💡 코칭 포인트: "Out of the question" means "absolutely impossible," which is too strong here. Also, when asking, flip the order: "should I...?" not "I need to...?"

→ "out of the question"은 '절대 안 돼'라는 강한 말이라 여기선 너무 세요. 물어볼 때는 should를 맨 앞으로 꺼내서 "Should I ~?" 모양으로 말하면 깔끔한 질문이 됩니다. 바로 앞 질문도 같은 원리예요: "What is the best animal do you like?" → "What animal do you like best?"



3. 🔧 고질적 문법 '한 놈만 패기' (Target Grammar)


Category: articles (a/an/the)

🎯 오늘의 패턴: 하나짜리 물건(apartment, park, picture) 앞에 a / the를 자꾸 빼먹고, 필요 없는 곳엔 a를 붙여요


오늘 Joey의 문장을 모아 보니, 이 습관이 가장 많이 반복됐습니다.


❌ Joey의 원문✅ 교정
"And in the middle of picture, there is the dog, a little big dog, and golden retriever."And in the middle of the picture, there's a dog, a pretty big one, a golden retriever.
"But in apartment, my wall is another wall."In an apartment, my wall is also my neighbor's wall.
"But yeah, I can see many cats when I was walking near park."I saw a lot of cats when I was walking near the park.
"so every member of family is a busy"Every member of the family is busy.
"dog is so activity animal"A dog is such an active animal.
"So we choose another pet like the fish or hamster?"So we'd choose another pet, like a fish or a hamster?

📌 핵심 규칙: 하나, 둘 셀 수 있는 물건이 혼자 있으면 앞에 꼭 a(아무거나 하나)나 the(그거, 우리 둘 다 아는 그것)를 붙이세요. 소리가 a, e, i, o, u로 시작하면 an이 돼요: an apartment, an active animal. busy, happy처럼 '어떤 상태'를 말하는 말 앞에는 a를 붙이지 않아요.

→ 소리로 기억하기: "near park"는 툭 끊겨 들리고, "near the park"는 리듬이 맞아떨어져요. 덩어리째 외우세요: *in the middle of the picture / in an apartment / near the park / a member of the family*


🔁 따라 읽기

4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)


1. low-maintenance /ˌloʊ ˈmeɪntənəns/ (adjective)

뜻: 손이 많이 안 가는, 관리가 쉬운
실전 예문: "This reporting system is pretty low-maintenance once it's set up."

2. commitment /kəˈmɪtmənt/ (noun)

뜻: (시간·노력을 쏟겠다는) 약속, 책임
실전 예문: "A long-term LNG contract is a big commitment for both sides."

3. stray /streɪ/ (adjective)

뜻: 길 잃은, 주인 없는 (stray dog / stray cat)
실전 예문: "There are a lot of stray cats near our office building."

4. feral /ˈfɪrəl/ (adjective)

뜻: 야생화된 (사람 손을 떠나 야생처럼 사는)
실전 예문: "Most of the cats in that park are feral, so they don't come near people."

5. accommodate /əˈkɑːmədeɪt/ (verb)

뜻: 수용하다, 맞춰 주다
실전 예문: "We can adjust the audit schedule to accommodate your team."
