## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "On the weekend, I gave the time to my wife."
   Natural: "Over the weekend, I gave my wife some time to herself."
2. I said: "I have some worry about that movement because my wife doesn't like the wind."
   Natural: "I have some concerns about the move because my wife doesn't like the wind."
3. I said: "My dream is to live in a house, but the reality is to live in an apartment."
   Natural: "My dream is to live in a house, but realistically we'll end up in an apartment."
4. I said: "I think we have the risk management teams. Our team name is the audit team, auditing department."
   Natural: "I think we have a risk management team. It's called the audit team, or the auditing department."
5. I said: "It is possible to understand going to the work or the path to the work."
   Natural: "It's about understanding the process or the workflow."
6. I said: "So I think in our team is only think about the internal process, internal teams."
   Natural: "So I think our team only focuses on internal processes and internal teams."
7. I said: "It is similar. Yeah, the salaryman. So maybe it's same. I work through. I go for work."
   Natural: "It's pretty much the same every day for a salaryman. I just go to work."
8. I said: "Oh yes, this picture I can see the focus on the hand, the zoom in the hand."
   Natural: "In this picture, I can see a close-up of the hand."
9. I said: "But sometimes I need to some writing because it is more convenient for me."

10. I said: "I guess they look like they're brainstorming or strategic planning in office ."

11. I said: "The purpose is to meet the goal, the efficiency for more best and more strategic."

12. I said: "Your explanation means that the plan is more big and huge . Yeah, more dreamy ."

13. I said: "The enterprise will die easier ."

14. I said: "I can see a man who is working on the desk. ... Yeah, sure. I'm done, teacher."

15. I said: "my favorite part is bonds and real estate."
   Natural: "My favorite types of investment are bonds and real estate."
16. I said: "our cash flow is limited because I'm a salaryman."

## Tutor's official corrections
1. "I do not yet"  ->  "I have not yet"
2. "I see a man who working on the desk"  ->  "I see a man who is working at the desk. I see a man whose work is on the desk."
3. "These days Korea government they offer some tax"  ->  "These days the Korean government offers some tax"
4. "Which is prefer"  ->  "Which do you prefer"
5. "I'm little conservation person"  ->  "I'm a little conservative. I'm a little bit of a conservative people."
6. "Five years ago almost people like the invest"  ->  "Five years ago [almost all/most] people like to invest in real estate"
7. "The stock market grows ups"  ->  "The stock market grows"
8. "DIS picture SHOW a professional group pf people"  ->  "THIS picture SHOW(S) a GROUP OF PROFESSIONAL people."
9. "It can help POCUS"  ->  "It can help you FOCUS (pronounced FOH-KUS)"
10. "What do you think is the purpose of STRATEGIC planning?"  ->  "What do you think is the purpose of STRATEGIC (pronounced STRAH-TEE-JIK) planning?"
11. "DUH arrrow needs to be DIRECT to the right way to find the TARGET."  ->  "The (pronounced THUH) arrow needs to be DIRECTED to the right way to find the TARGET."
12. "Today I want to talk today topic"  ->  "Today I want to talk about today's topic"

15. "What is your favorite supplier in office"  ->  "What is your favorite office supply"

17. "Sometimes that is I need more directly and more classic"  ->  "Sometimes I need to write more immediate and classic"

20. "What are DEY?"  ->  "What are THEY?"

22. "Saturday will went to hotel pool"  ->  "Saturday we went to the hotel pool"

## Key vocabulary
- assess (동사) — 평가하다, 사정하다
- contingency plan (명사) — 비상 계획, 만일의 사태에 대비한 계획
- insulate (동사) — (영향으로부터) 보호하다, 고립시키다
- rigorous (형용사) — 철저한, 엄격한, 정밀한
- redundancy (명사) — 불필요한 중복, 여분
- stationery (명사) — 문구류 (종이, 펜, 봉투 등 글쓰기 관련 용품)
- office supplies (명사) — 사무용품 (사무실에서 사용하는 모든 소모품, 문구류 포함 더 넓은 개념)
- clipboard (명사) — 클립보드 (서류를 고정하는 판)
- jot down (동사구) — (간단히) 적다, 메모하다
- corporate card (명사) — 법인 카드 (회사 경비를 지불하는 데 사용되는 카드)
- strategic (형용사) — 전략적인, 중요한 계획과 관련된
- brainstorming (명사) — 브레인스토밍 (새로운 아이디어를 얻기 위한 토론)
- oversight (명사) — 감독, 관리, 감시
- delegate (동사) — (권한·업무 등을) 위임하다
- visionary (형용사) — 선견지명이 있는, 비전이 있는
