## Corrections — what I said vs. the natural version
1. I said: "I have to interview at dinner time, so yeah, so I need to change my schedule."
   Natural: "I have an interview scheduled for dinner time, so I need to change my schedule."
2. I said: "After work, if we have an interview, at that time it is kept secret to the investor or research."
   Natural: "If we conduct an interview after work, it's kept confidential from investors or for research purposes."
3. I said: "Do you have an experience to take an ambulance or a helicopter?"
   Natural: "Have you ever taken an ambulance or a helicopter?"
4. I said: "I'm doing traveling."
   Natural: "I'm traveling."
5. I said: "That name is the gathering factory."
   Natural: "That city is known for its factories."
6. I said: "It is possible to pre-talking if you need to just recover your time, your eyes at the time. You just pre-talking. Yeah. And close your eyes and talking to me."
   Natural: "It's possible to talk beforehand if you need time to recover. You can just close your eyes and talk to me."
7. I said: "I came back to my office and finished my work travel. So it's good and I came back to normal days, the routine days."
   Natural: "I'm back in the office after my work trip, so I'm glad to be back to my usual routine."
8. I said: "my son doesn't have a residency proof, so usually use the passport in local or abroad."
   Natural: "My son doesn't have proof of residency, so he usually uses his passport both locally and abroad."
9. I said: "So they check the same person or not. Is it fake ID? Is it fake passport? So they confirm everything. So it is wrong, better wrong."
   Natural: "So they check to see if it's the same person and if the ID or passport is fake. They confirm everything, which often takes a long time."

## Key vocabulary
- audit (명사, 동사) — 회계 감사, 심사; (장부 등을) 감사하다, 심사하다
- confidential (형용사) — 기밀의, 비밀의
- reschedule (동사) — 일정을 변경하다, 다시 잡다

- responsive (형용사) — 즉각 반응하는, 반응이 빠른; (요구에) 부응하는

- valid (형용사) — 유효한, 타당한
