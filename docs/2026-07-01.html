항목 1
❌ As Said: "But usually in Seoul, the common transportation is subway because it is connected like a web, a spider web."
✅ Natural: "But in Seoul, the most common transportation is the subway, as it's interconnected like a spider web."
💎 Professional: "In Seoul, the subway is the predominant mode of transport, given its extensive, web-like network."
💡 Coaching Point: Using "most common" or "predominant mode of transport" sounds more natural and professional than "common transportation." Also, "as it's interconnected" or "given its extensive network" provides a smoother connection than "because it is connected."

→ "most common" 또는 "predominant mode of transport"를 사용하면 "common transportation"보다 더 자연스럽고 전문적으로 들립니다. 또한, "as it's interconnected" 또는 "given its extensive network"와 같은 표현은 "because it is connected"보다 더 부드러운 연결을 제공합니다.


항목 2
❌ As Said: "So usually I love to take the bus better than the subway."
✅ Natural: "So I usually prefer taking the bus over the subway."
💎 Professional: "Consequently, I generally opt for the bus rather than the subway."
💡 Coaching Point: "Prefer X over Y" is a more idiomatic and common way to express preference than "like X better than Y." "Opt for" is a more formal verb suitable for professional contexts.

→ "Prefer X over Y"는 "like X better than Y"보다 선호도를 표현하는 더 관용적이고 일반적인 방식입니다. "Opt for"는 전문적인 맥락에 적합한 더 격식 있는 동사입니다.


항목 3
❌ As Said: "I think the first I consider that work is about company or my work. I divide first."
✅ Natural: "I first consider whether the appointment is for the company or for myself. I'll divide them first."
💎 Professional: "My initial consideration is whether the appointment pertains to company matters or personal affairs; I categorize them accordingly."
💡 Coaching Point: The original phrasing is a bit indirect. "Consider whether X is for A or B" is clearer. Adding "I'll divide them first" or "I categorize them accordingly" makes the action of separating the tasks more explicit and natural.

→ 원래 표현은 다소 간접적입니다. "Consider whether X is for A or B"가 더 명확합니다. "I'll divide them first" 또는 "I categorize them accordingly"를 추가하면 업무를 구분하는 행동을 더 명확하고 자연스럽게 만듭니다.

4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)


오늘 대화에서 나왔거나 맥락상 유용했던 단어 5가지입니다.


1. interconnected /ˌɪntərkəˈnektɪd/ (adjective)
뜻: 서로 연결된, 상호 관련된
실전 예문: "Our company's departments need to be more interconnected for better collaboration."
2. predominant /prɪˈdɑːmənənt/ (adjective)
뜻: 우세한, 지배적인, 주요한
실전 예문: "The predominant color in this design is blue."
3. idiomatic /ˌɪdiəˈmætɪk/ (adjective)
뜻: 관용적인, 숙어의
실전 예문: "To sound more natural, it's important to learn idiomatic expressions."
4. pertains to /pərˈteɪnz tuː/ (verb phrase)
뜻: ~에 관한 것이다, ~에 속하다
실전 예문: "Please only ask questions that pertain to the project scope."
5. consequently /ˌkɑːnsəˈkwentli/ (adverb)
뜻: 결과적으로, 따라서
실전 예문: "The market demand increased significantly; consequently, production had to be ramped up."
