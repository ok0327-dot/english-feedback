2. 💬 유창성 업그레이드 (Fluency & Natural Flow)


Joey가 사용한 표현 중 의미는 충분히 전달되었지만, 조금 더 자연스럽거나 격식 있게 다듬을 수 있는 표현 3가지를 골라봤어요.


1st Expression
❌ As Said: "So the temperature is still high, but it's getting a little lower, going low."
✅ Natural: "So the temperature is still high, but it's starting to cool down a bit."
💎 Professional: "While the temperature remains high, we are beginning to experience a slight decrease."
💡 Coaching Point: "Starting to cool down" is a more common and idiomatic way to describe a gradual change in weather. "Going low" is a bit too direct and less nuanced.

→ "starting to cool down"은 날씨가 점차 시원해지는 것을 묘사할 때 더 흔하고 자연스러운 표현입니다. "going low"는 너무 직접적이고 뉘앙스가 부족합니다.


2nd Expression
❌ As Said: "Yeah, that's normal, but it's so special for me. It's so nice."
✅ Natural: "It was a simple outing, but it felt very special to me."
💎 Professional: "Although it was a routine activity, it held significant personal meaning for me."
💡 Coaching Point: "Felt special" or "meant a lot to me" conveys the emotional value more effectively than just saying "it's so special." Adding context like "simple outing" or "routine activity" makes it more descriptive.

→ "Felt special" 또는 "meant a lot to me"는 단순히 "it's so special"이라고 말하는 것보다 감정적인 가치를 더 효과적으로 전달합니다. "simple outing" 또는 "routine activity"와 같은 맥락을 추가하면 더 구체적으로 묘사할 수 있습니다.


3rd Expression
❌ As Said: "So if I want, I will go to a different beach, because we have different beaches."
✅ Natural: "So I can visit a different beach whenever I want, because there are many."
💎 Professional: "I have the flexibility to visit various beaches at my discretion, given their abundance."
💡 Coaching Point: "Whenever I want" is a more natural phrasing for expressing recurring possibility than "if I want, I will go." "There are many" is a simpler way to say "we have different beaches" in this context.

→ "Whenever I want"는 "if I want, I will go"보다 반복적인 가능성을 표현할 때 더 자연스러운 구문입니다. "There are many"는 이 맥락에서 "we have different beaches"를 말하는 더 간단한 방법입니다.

4. 📗 어휘 확장 (Vocabulary Vault)


수업에서 나왔거나 맥락상 유용한 단어 5가지입니다.


1. Heirloom /ˈɛr.luːm/ (명사)
뜻: 가보, 비품 (대대로 물려 내려오는 귀중품)
실전 예문: "This antique watch has been a family heirloom for generations."

2. Family resemblance /ˌfæm.ə.li rɪˈzɛm.bləns/ (명사구)
뜻: 가족 간의 닮은 모습, 유전적 유사성
실전 예문: "There's a strong family resemblance between my brother and our father."

3. Pass down /pæs daʊn/ (동사구)
뜻: (전통, 유산, 지식 등을) 다음 세대로 물려주다
실전 예문: "We hope to pass down our cultural heritage to our children."

4. Tight-knit /ˈtaɪt.nɪt/ (형용사)
뜻: (가족, 공동체 등이) 사이가 매우 좋은, 긴밀한
실전 예문: "They come from a very tight-knit community where everyone supports each other."

5. Look up to /lʊk ˈʌp tuː/ (동사구)
뜻: (누구를) 존경하다, 우러러보다
실전 예문: "Many young athletes look up to professional sports stars."

5. 📝 실전 복습 챌린지 (Practice)


오늘 배운 표현과 문법을 활용하여 영작 연습을 해봅시다!


1. 상황: 당신의 할머니께서 대대로 물려 내려오는 오래된 보석함을 가지고 계십니다.

영작: "My grandmother has an old jewelry box that has been a family heirloom for generations."

3. 상황: 친구들이 긴 연휴에 여행을 가자고 제안했지만, 당신은 가족과 함께 시간을 보내는 것을 더 중요하게 생각합니다.

영작: "Even though my friends invited me on a trip during the long holiday, I chose to spend time with my family because I prioritize spending time with them."

*(힌트: '가족과 시간을 보내는 것을 더 중요하게 생각한다'는 의미를 명확히 전달해 보세요.)*
